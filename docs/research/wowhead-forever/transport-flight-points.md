# Flight points, ships, zeppelins, tram (guide 35352)

 There are numerous methods for traveling around Azeroth in World of Warcraft: Forever. This guide covers the locations and routes of flight points, ships, zeppelins, and the Deeprun Tram. Flight Points, Ships, and Zeppelins in Forever Using all of the transport methods available to you can save huge amounts of time, so knowing all the options is important. Flight Points in Forever Flight Masters can be found around the world; they are either Alliance-only, Horde-only, or neutral. Talking to a Flight Master unlocks their flight point and allows you to travel to, through, and from there in the future. Flight Masters that you haven't unlocked show a green ! above their head. Each flight point has routes to other nearby flight points, allowing you to travel between them by talking to the Flight Master. If you want to fly between two distant flight points, you must have unlocked connected flight points between the two. Taking a flight path costs silver; the further away your destination is, the more expensive it will be. While flying, a Request Stop button will appear. If you press this, you will be dropped off at the next flight point, rather than completing the entire route. This only works when there is a flight point between you and your final destination. For example, if you are flying from Splintertree to Dustwallow, the route goes through Crossroads. If you press Request Stop before reaching Crossroads, you will be dropped off there. If you press it after passing Crossroads, you will be dropped off at Dustwallow. Ships and Airships in Forever Ships travel between various docks around the world. They usually arrive every 3-5 minutes and depart after waiting for around a minute. Traveling on a ship is free. To take a ship, all you need to do is head to the dock and walk on when it arrives. Most ships are intended for Alliance players, though there are a couple of neutral ships. Horde players can travel on any ship, though guards may make it difficult for them. Zeppelins in Forever Zeppelins function similarly to ships. They travel back and forth between towers, arriving every 3-5 minutes and waiting for a minute for players to get on. Traveling on a zeppelin is free. To travel on a zeppelin, climb to the top of the tower and walk on when it arrives. Zeppelins are intended for Horde players; Alliance players can use them if they can get on, but guards will attack them. Deeprun Tram in Forever The Deeprun Tram runs between Ironforge and Stormwind. Players of either faction can use it for free, but as these are two Alliance cities, Horde players will find it extremely difficult to use. A tram arrives on one of the two tracks every minute, waits for 10 seconds, then departs.
