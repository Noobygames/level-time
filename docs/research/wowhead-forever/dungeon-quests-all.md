## Source: https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide=34916

 Dungeon quests in WoW: Forever are a great source of experience and high-quality loot. This guide presents a complete list of all dungeon quests, including requirements and prerequisites, how difficult it is to obtain the quest, where to pick it up, and whether that quest is shareable. We also include a table of the optimal levels to enter a dungeon. Several dungeons require keys and other unique items to access areas, take advantage of shortcuts, or summon certain bosses. We have noted where such keys are required, whether Lockpicking/Seaforium Charges will circumvent them, and links to more information. Guide Key Header Code Description Level ## Minimum level to pick up that quest Side Marked accordingly if faction restricted Effort Minimal effort, 0-1 prerequisites, usually directly sharable 2-3 prerequisites and/or more travel involved Requires longer and/or more difficult quest chains to qualify Share? Shareable with all party members who meet minimum requirements. If the check is a different color, it's only sharable with the same class. Not shareable; often requires getting an item from quest giver Pre Has prerequisite quests that must be completed and turned in first; can often be shared with others who have completed those prerequisites Drop Can only be taken by party members who have looted that drop. Some drops have prerequisite quests you must do to get the drop; these are marked both Pre and Drop . Esc Escort quest that must be completed by entire party Some quests are also marked as Paladin Only , Mage Only , etc. These can only be picked up, shared with, and completed by members of the same class. There is also one quest that is only for Blacksmiths.
