## Guide text
Source: https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide=35227

Dire Maul [zone=2557] is a sprawling dungeon hub located in Feralas. This guide provides tips and strategies for completing the Capital Gardens of Dire Maul, including everything you need before and during the dungeon, quests to complete inside, boss strategies, loot, and more.

If you are new to WoW: Forever, please look out for this symbol throughout the guide for additional, beginner-friendly tips.

## Dire Maul Capital Gardens Overview in World of Warcraft: Forever

The recommended level for Dire Maul Capital Gardens is between 54 and 60. In-game players usually refer to the Capital Gardens as "Gardens," however, some also call it "Dire Maul West." 

## Location of Dire Maul Capital Gardens in World of Warcraft: Forever

Dire Maul is north of The High Wilderness in Feralas [zone=357]. It is easily accessible to both Alliance and Horde players.

Alliance players can fly into Thalanaar, at the eastern border of Feralas where it meets Thousand Needles [zone=400], and travel West along the main road, being sure to skirt around Camp Mojache. Alternatively, Alliance can reach Dire Maul by flying in to Feathermoon Stronghold, taking the boat or swimming directly east, and following the road East to Dire Maul.

Horde players can fly into Camp Mojache and travel West along the main road. 

When you're partway across the The High Wilderness, you'll notice a junction to the North. This will lead you into Dire Maul.

To reach the Capital Gardens, make your way through the complex to the Maul arena. The portal to the Capital Gardens is in a recess on the West wall. 

As with many World of Warcraft: Forever Dungeons, wiping (the entire group dying) in Dire Maul Capital Gardens can be a frustrating time. 

Ideally, if the entire group is not dead, you should be able to get a rez (resurrection). This is a spell someone casts on you to bring your character back to life with a small amount of health and mana. Priests and Shamans learn this early; Paladins need to complete a quest to learn resurrection and may not have it. Druids only have an in-combat resurrection spell and are unlikely to train it before Dire Maul Capital Gardens.

If you can't get a rez, click to release your spirit, then run back to the dungeon from the Spirit Healer as a ghost. You collect your body when you enter the instance.

## Dire Maul Capital Gardens Bosses in World of Warcraft: Forever

Make sure you clear all of the ancients in the area, otherwise they will all aggro when you pull Tendris Warpwood [npc=11489].

Tendris Warpwood [npc=11489] is an ancient boss located in Dire Maul West.

Abilities

- Entangle [spell=22994]
- Grasping Vines [spell=22924]
- Trample [spell=5568]
- Uppercut [spell=22916]

Strategy

Watch out for his AoE melee knockdown, Trample [spell=5568]. Ranged, be sure to stand back to avoid this. Entangle [spell=22994] is a root effect that can be dispelled. Wait for your tank to gain threat and DPS him down.

Once Tendris Warpwood [npc=11489] has fallen, head back to take the northern doorway and stick to the northeast corner of the room. Patrol around carefully, as the Eldreth Spirit [npc=11472] are invisible. A Warlock {/forever/class=9/warlock}'s Detect Invisibility [spell=11743] works nicely if you have. Proceed onward to find a ramp, and clear the entire path leading up to it. Ascend the ramp, and you'll find Magister Kalendris [npc=11487].

Magister Kalendris [npc=11487] is a shadow priest-type boss located in Dire Maul West.

Abilities

- Dominate Mind [spell=7645]
- Mind Blast [spell=15587]
- Mind Flay [spell=22919]
- Shadow Word: Pain [spell=15654]
- Shadowform [spell=22917]

Strategy

Pretend you're fighting a Shadow Priest {/forever/class=5/priest}. Interupt his spells. Dispell any magic. Use any sort of Shadow Resist such as Shadow Resistance Aura [spell=19896] or Prayer of Shadow Protection [spell=27683] and burn him down.

Afterwards, continue clearing your way through the room and make your way towards the mana pylon. Keep clearing trash and you'll find Shen'dralar Ancient [npc=14358], who gives you the quest The Madness Within [quest=7461], to kill Immol'thar [npc=11496] & Prince Tortheldrin [npc=11486]. Head to the upper floor of The Court of the Highborne to find Illyanna Ravenoak [npc=11488].

Tsu'zee [npc=11467] is a rogue-type silver elite found in the Court of the Highborne, near the ramps, in Dire Maul West.

Abilities

- Backstab [spell=15657]
- Blind [spell=21060]
- Gouge [spell=12540]
- Sinister Strike [spell=15667]

Strategy

Fighting Tsu'zee [npc=11467] is like encountering a Rogue {/forever/class=4/rogue}, and she hits pretty hard. Make sure your tank gains aggro before attacking. Dispel Blind [spell=21060] from your party if able to, and burn her down.

Illyanna Ravenoak [npc=11488] is a Hunter-type boss located in Dire Maul West.

Abilities

- Aimed Shot [spell=20904]
- Concussive Shot [spell=22914]
- Immolation Trap [spell=22910]
- Volley [spell=22908]

Strategy

Illyanna Ravenoak [npc=11488] is just like fighting a Hunter {/forever/class=3/hunter}, accompanied with a pet bear, Ferra [npc=14308]. All of her abilities, minus Immolation Trap [spell=22910], can be negated simply by standing in melee range of her, similar to that of a Hunter {/forever/class=3/hunter}. Most groups tend to burn down Illyanna Ravenoak [npc=11488] first, then kill Ferra [npc=14308] after.

Once they have fallen, head down to the center of the Court of the Highborne and you'll find a locked door. Crescent Key [item=18249] or a Rogue {/forever/class=4/rogue} with high enough skill in Lockpicking {/forever/skill=633/lockpicking} is required to advance. When the door is opened, you'll find yourself down a tunnel leading to Immol'thar [npc=11496]'s Prison.

There are many patrolling packs of mobs in this room, so you must be very careful with your pulls. A Warlock {/forever/class=9/warlock}'s Banish [spell=18647] is incredibly useful here. You must clear around all of the mana pylons for the shield to drop and to proceed to engage Immol'thar [npc=11496].

Immol'thar [npc=11496] is a beast boss located in Dire Maul West.

Abilities

- Enrage [spell=8269]
- Eye of Immol'thar [spell=22899]
- Infected Bite [spell=16128]
- Portal of Immol'thar [spell=22950]
- Trample [spell=5568]

Strategy

Immol'thar [npc=11496] will summon eyes around the room. Designate someone, preferably a ranged attacker, to dispose of these. He will Enrage [spell=8269] at 50% health, so healers: get ready for big heals on the tank! He will also randomly teleport players to the middle of the room. If this happens to your tank, be wary and allow for the tank to return and reestablish aggro.

If you have the Warlock {/forever/class=9/warlock} quest, Dreadsteed of Xoroth [quest=7631], now is the time to do it. For a detailed guide on the Warlock Dreadsteed, visit: Classic WoW Warlock Felsteed & Dreadsteed Guide.

Now that Immol'thar [npc=11496] has fallen, it's time to proceed to the final boss, Prince Tortheldrin [npc=11486]. Follow the hallway into the Athenaeum. If you need to restock & repair, there is a Shen'dralar Provisioner [npc=14371] in the southeast corner of the room. Immol'thar [npc=11496] is the southwest corner of the room.

Immol'thar [npc=11496] is the final boss of Dire Maul West.

Abilities

- Arcane Blast [spell=22920]
- Counterspell [spell=20537]
- Summon [spell=22995]
- Whirlwind [spell=15589]

Strategy

Prince Tortheldrin [npc=11486] hits pretty hard and fast. Tank him in the corner to avoid his knockback, Arcane Blast [spell=22920]. Be aware that after he does cast Arcane Blast [spell=22920] his aggro resets. Cease DPS until your tank gets him back, then burn him down fast.

Lord Hel'nurath [npc=14506] is a special boss Dire Maul West. This boss can only be summoned by Warlocks who possess the three summoning items obtained during their Epic Mount Questline: J'eevee's Jar [item=18663], Black Lodestone [item=18629] and Xorothian Glyphs [item=18670].

Abilities

- Cripple [spell=20812]
- Shadow Bolt Volley [spell=22665]

Strategy

The hardest part of the Lord Hel'nurath [npc=14506] fight is the ritual leading to him. The ritual to summon Lord Hel'nurath happens at the altar where Immol'thar [npc=11496] is originally located, after killing the boss. The group must survive through many waves of demons while ensuring the Warlock is able to maintain all three ritual items active, as they grant beneficial buffs and aid you with killing waves. The waves will mainly consist of Imps and an occasional Dread Guard [npc=14483], which Warlocks can either Banish [spell=18647] or Subjugate Demon [spell=11726]. The event will last around 6 minutes, and once it ends, the remaining demons will despawn and Immol'thar's altar will glow with shadow power.

Take this pause to heal and drink, and once the group is ready, use the Xorothian Glyphs [item=18670] to invoke a Xorothian Dreadsteed [npc=14502]. Once the Horse is at 50%, Lord Hel'nurath [npc=14506] will appear: His abilities aren't too tough and he does not hit terribly hard, but it is still best to dispose of him before the Dreadsteed.
