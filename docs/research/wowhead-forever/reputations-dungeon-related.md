 This guide covers the basics of the Shen'dralar reputation, how to obtain the class trinkets provided by the Shen'dralar, and their libram enchants. Shen'dralar Overview in WoW: Forever The Shen'dralar in WoW: Forever are an obscure and secretive faction of Highborne who dwell within the Athenaeum of Dire Maul . Shen'dralar Location in WoW: Forever The Shen'dralar do not have a quartermaster per se. The entirety of their faction is located within the library in the Athenaeum area of Dire Maul. You can access the Athenaeum from both Dire Maul West and Dire Maul North. Both entrances require the Crescent Key , or another way to unlock doors: Rogues can Pick Lock the door (275 Lockpicking required), or Pick Pocket the key from Mol'dar. Engineers can open the door with a Powerful Seaforium Charge . Blacksmiths can open the door with an Arcanite Skeleton Key . Entering from Dire Maul West In the West area of Dire Maul, you can enter the Athenaeum from the northeastern exit in Immol'thar 's room. You actually need to enter the Athenaeum in order to find and defeat Prince Tortheldrin , the final boss of Dire Maul West. Entering from Dire Maul North In the North area of Dire Maul, the entrance is right by the entrance to the dungeon, up a ramp by Guard Mol'dar 's area. You can reach this area with minimal aggro from any mobs if you're careful. How to Earn Shen'dralar Reputation in WoW: Forever All Shen'dralar reputation comes from quests turned in at the Athenaeum. Each source is covered in more detail under Reputation Rewards below. In World of Warcraft: Forever, items are obfuscated until they are found. Shen'dralar Lore The Shen'dralar were once the most respected arcanists in Queen Azshara's court, and they were trusted with keeping her most precious spell tomes safe. They needed somewhere secure to store them, so they traveled to the southern jungles of Kalimdor and built a grand city there: Eldre'Thalas. Eldre'Thalas and the Shen'dralar survived the War of the Ancients, but it came at a cost. Without the Well of Eternity to draw from, their immortality began to fade, and they were left isolated and slowly withering away. Their leader, Prince Tortheldrin, came up with a solution. Using the pylons housed within Eldre'Thalas, he summoned and bound a terrifying demon, Immol'thar, and siphoned its energy to keep his followers going. This worked for a while; over time, though, keeping Immol'thar imprisoned while also sustaining life in Eldre'Thalas took more power than the city had. Prince Tortheldrin's answer was to turn on his own people. He and his loyalists began murdering fellow Highborne and using their lives to fuel the pylons and preserve their own immortality. Some Highborne managed to escape this grisly end, but many did not, and their spirits now roam Dire Maul.

Three Gelkis-only quests reward your choice of one of the recipes for Enchanting, Leatherworking, and Tailoring. Stonewrought Design is in Blackrock Mountain. 10. Magma or Lava? is in Blackrock Mountain. Silverpine Forest, Alterac Mountains, and Scholomance: 3 books You can get these three in one trip north. Note that there's now a boat that stops at Southshore! The Dalaran Digest, Vol. 23 is in Ambermill, Silverpine Forest. Easy, and the mobs are not hostile to you! 12. Defensive Magics 101 is in Alterac Mountains. Cheese it by clicking through the wall. Just swim to the island, which is empty of mobs. Swamp of Sorrows and Badlands: 2 books Swamp of Sorrows and Badlands can both be retrieved in one go, while also grabbing Nethergarde's flight point. Beware of Horde guards on the road in SOS which will flag and kill you. 14. Sanguine Sorcery is in Swamp of Sorrows. Lots of corpse running to get to the temple, but the pickup itself is easy. 15. Mummies: A Guide to the Unsavory Undead is in Badlands. The road is somewhat safe, but picking up the book will require a corpse run. Resurrect and click the book. The Barrens, Thousand Needles, and Stonetalon Mountains: 5 books This is a BIG trip. While doing this you can also prog the Sleeping Bag quest chain (which starts in Westfall). If you don't have Booty Bay's flight point (why would you?), swim from the west coast of Westfall to Booty Bay (grab the FP there), then take the boat to Ratchet. Grab the FP in Ratchet, grab the book at Ratchet, then head down to Thousand Needles for another book (note: level 28 mobs are here), and unlock the FP in Thalanaar. Then take the FP to Ratchet, gather the book in the northern Barrens, then the one in Wailing Caverns (central Barrens), then head West to Stonetalon to grab another book (and prog your Sleeping Bag quest if you haven't already). Then head up and grab the FP in the north of Stonetalon. 16. Arcanic Systems Manual is in the Barrens. 17. Baxtan: On Destructive Magics is in Ratchet, in the Barrens. 18.
