 Riverglades is a new zone added to World of Warcraft: Forever. This guide will cover how to get there, factions, and dungeons in this zone. Overview of Riverglades Riverglades is a rugged frontier of rivers, grasslands, trade routes, struggling communities, and old grudges. It is a contested zone adjacent to Badlands , Burning Steppes , and Redridge Mountains on the eastern coastline of the Eastern Kingdom. The level range for Riverglades is 36-44 . The main Alliance hub is Farholde Keep , found to the south. The main Horde hub is Rog'mar , in the center of the zone. There is one dungeon in Riverglades: Krol'dok Stronghold . Map of Riverglades How to get to Riverglades Players can access Riverglades on foot by taking the road in the north of the Redridge Mountains or the south of the Badlands. Alternatively, a new boat travels between Steamwheedle Port in Tanaris and Powderfuse Port in Riverglades. Horde: Grakna is at Rog'mar (/way 59.6 45.0). Each quest offers around the same Experience as a Dungeon Quest would. These former riders started anew in the unexplored lands along the eastern coast, establishing their home, new relationships, and trade routes. Horde: Bolder'ok Clan were exiled for betraying their false prophets. These former members of the Twilight's Hammer seek to forge their own path. Players will assist their faction in their conflict against the other faction and the Twilight's Hammer, completing quests and earning rewards along the way.  Zephras Isle is a new zone added in World of Warcraft: Forever. This guide will cover how to get there, where to find trainers, and notable quest rewards in this zone. Overview of Zephras Isle Zephras Isle is the starting zone for Skyborne characters. It is intended for players from levels 1 to 12. Access to the Skyborne race and the Zephras Isle starting experience questline requires the purchase of the Skyborne Heroic pack or a higher edition , but players can go to the island without any purchase. Map of Zephras Isle How to get to and From Zephras Isle Two skyboats arrive about every 5 minutes and take players to and from Valanaar on Zephras Isle. One skyboat goes to Dalaran, and the other to just north of Thunder Bluff. Players of both factions can take either skyboat, though generally the Dalaran route is intended for Alliance players and the Thunder Bluff route for Horde players. Reputation Factions on Zephras Isle Zephras Isle has two reputation factions: The Alliance-sided High Order are the Skyborne descendants of the Highborne magisters of Eldre'Thalas. The Horde-sided Windshapers are shamanistic spiritual leaders of the Skyborne people on Zephras Isle. Most quests on the isle reward reputation with whichever is associated with your character's faction.
