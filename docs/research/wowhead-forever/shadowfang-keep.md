### Journal bosses

#### Rethilgore (NPC ids [3914])
Overview: Rethilgore is a tank and spank in the truest sense of the word. His only ability, Soul Drain, is negligible. He also has a moderately low health pool and a Loot table comparable to his difficulty. The only thing you should be aware of is the mobs surrounding him, 2 of which should be pulled prior to engaging the boss. The one mob that will remain, a Bleak Worg, should be polymorphed or burned down first prior to switching DPS to Rethilgore.
Role notes: tank: Clear all the mobs you can before pulling the boss. The only mob that can't be pulled without the boss is a Bleak Worg.; damage: CC or kill the add before killing the boss.
- Soul Drain [spell=7295]: Immobilizes an enemy for 10 sec. and drains 35 health, transferring it to the caster.
Loot: Rugged Spaulders [item=5254], Sorcerer Collar [item=273457], Cell Keeper's Claws [item=273456]

#### Fel Steeds / Shadow Charger (NPC ids [3864, 3865])
Overview: The Fel Steed and Shadow Charger deal an incredibly high amount of damage, and pulling one of them will pull all three, regardless of how carefully you pull the first mob. Try to CC at least one of the horses and, if you have a Hunter or Warlock, pet-tank the second while your main tank and DPS are focused on the third before cycling to the next mob. Your healer should be aware of the massive amount of damage that will be incoming during this encounter.
Role notes: healer: Be prepared to heal a lot of damage.; damage: CC/pet-tank horses that you aren't killing yet.
- Fel Stomp [spell=7139]: Inflicts normal damage plus 20 to nearby enemies, stunning them for 3 sec. and increasing the Physical damage they take by 10.
Loot: Eerie Stable Lantern [item=6341], Fel Steed Saddlebags [item=932]

#### Baron Silverlaine (NPC ids [3887])
Overview: What makes Baron Silverlaine challenging is his higher level (24) and his primary ability, Veil of Shadow. Make sure your hybrid classes are assisting the healer here with tank healing, as the tank will go down quickly otherwise. Aside from that, the fight is a tank and spank.

#### Commander Springvale (NPC ids [4278])
Overview: Commander Springvale is flanked by two mobs that will pull with him. The first is a Haunted Servitor, which should be burned down quickly. The second is a Wailing Guardsman, which would be pulled by either a Hunter or Warlock pet away from the group, as it can cast an AoE silence that can potentially wipe the party. Make sure to prioritize Springvale after the Servitor is killed, and interrupt his healing spells. Once he's down, take out the guardsman, who again should be tanked off to the side by either a pet or possibly a Warrior/Druid offtank.
Role notes: healer: Stay away from the Wailing Guardsman, as it can silence nearby players.; damage: Kill the Haunted Servitor first.

## Shadowfang Keep Overview in World of Warcraft: Forever

The recommended level for Shadowfang Keep is between 22 and 30. In-game players usually refer to Shadowfang Keep as "SFK".

## Location of Shadowfang Keep in World of Warcraft: Forever

Shadowfang Keep is located in the Horde leveling zone of Silverpine Forest [zone=130]. The instance portal is just north of Pyrewood Village. While SFK is easily accessible by Horde players, the Alliance will have to travel far and walk a long way to get there. 

For Horde , fly to The Sepulcher in Silverpine Forest [zone=130] and travel south along the road, then west until you hit the Keep. Newer classes that are Non-Undead can take the Zeppelin from Orgrimmar to Tirisfal Glades and make their way to the dungeon from there.

For Alliance is a bit of a journey. A newer player's fastest and safest travel will be taking a series of boats. Skyborne can take a teleport to Dalaran from Stormwind City's Mage Tower, and then cut through Ambermill in Silverpine Forest.

- Take the boat to Auberdine from Stormwind Harbor 
- Once the boat lands in Auberdine, go to the opposite side of the port to take the boat to Menethil Harbor
- Stay on this Boat until it takes you to Southshore in Hillsbrad Foothills
- Follow the road from Southshore to Silverpine Forest into Shadowfang Keep

As with many World of Warcraft: Forever Dungeons, wiping (the entire group dying) in Shadowfang Keep can be a frustrating time. 

Ideally, if the entire group is not dead, you should be able to get a rez (resurrection). This is a spell someone casts on you to bring your character back to life with a small amount of health and mana. Priests and Shamans learn this early; Paladins need to complete a quest to learn resurrection and may not have it. Druids only have an in-combat resurrection spell and are unlikely to train it before Shadowfang Keep.

If you can't get a rez, click to release your spirit, then run back to the dungeon from the Spirit Healer (marked on the map below) as a ghost. You collect your body when you enter the instance.

Horde's Spirit Healer is located at the Sepulcher in Silverpine Forest

Alliance's Spirit Healer is located at the Southshore in Hillsbrad Foothills.

## Noteworthy Shadowfang Keep Trash Mobs in World of Warcraft: Forever

### Shadowfang Moonwalker
The Shadowfang Moonwalker [npc=3853] is a Worgen-like mob located primarily in the courtyard of Shadowfang Keep. They have one notable ability, Anti-Magic Shield [spell=24021], which makes them immune to all magical spells for 10 seconds. 

Strategy: The Strategy to deal with the Moonwalkers is less about gameplay and more about group composition. Make sure to bring at least one additional melee class aside from the tank to this dungeon. Two extra melees would be even better to burn down the Moonwalkers faster during their magic immunity phase. 

### Wailing Guardsman
Wailing Guardsman [npc=3877] is a ghostly humanoid mob that can be found on the upper levels of Shadowfang Keep. They have one primary ability, Screams of the Past [spell=7074], which silences all players within 5 yards for 5 seconds. 

Strategy:  Make sure your spellcasting classes remain at least 5 yards away from the guardsmen, especially your healer, so that they do not get silenced.

## The Tome of Dalaran Location in WoW: Forever

In WoW: Forever, located in Arugal's room, is the Black Tome [item=276561]. It is incredibly important that all players loot this tome, as it is needed to summon an additional boss in a new dungeon: City of Dalaran [zone=16544]. Players will take the tome to Ambermill in the town hall and link it to the Leyline focus to create the Tome of Dalaran [item=275989].
