World of Warcraft: Forever releases with nine new dungeons, alongside every dungeon from Classic. This guide covers what each dungeon is, its level range, and where to find it.

## New and Existing Dungeon Levels in WoW: Forever

The nine new dungeons in Forever are all available on release, along with all previous dungeons from Classic. The new dungeons fill the gaps between the Classic level brackets, smoothing out the leveling process, with The Hall of Thanes and Ruins of Lordaeron both available before level 20.

## Dungeon Party Roles in WoW: Forever

Dungeons are usually completed with five players: one tank, one healer, and three DPS.

### Tanks
Tanks hold threat, absorb damage, and stop enemies from killing the DPS and healer. Usually, a Warrior, Paladin or Druid Tanks.

### DPS
All classes can deal DPS, but the most popular dungeon DPS are Mages, Warriors, Rogues, Warlocks, and Hunters. Hybrid classes can DPS too, but often end up healing when leveling.

### Healers
A healer keeps the party alive and resurrects dead players. This is a Priest, Paladin, Shaman, or Druid.

## Dungeon Party Sizes in WoW: Forever

All dungeons allow you to enter with a raid of 10 players, except Blackrock Depths, Stratholme (both sides), and Scholomance, which limit you to 5 players. It is possible that the new Forever dungeons may be capped; we do not know yet.

Party quests cannot be completed in a raid group, so if you plan to complete dungeon quests, make sure there are only 5 players in your group.

## Leveling Through Dungeons in WoW: Forever

Dungeon Grinding is less effective in WoW: Forever than it has been in other expansions. This is because the Dungeon Experience has been greatly reduced. Initially, this was made up for with additional dungeon quest experience; however, that was also deemed too much and has since been reduced too. 

### Melee Cleave Dungeon Leveling
Melee Cleave groups are built around Warriors, who deal high damage to multiple enemies with Cleave [spell=845], Whirlwind [spell=1680], and Sweeping Strikes [spell=12292], and have little downtime thanks to Rage. Shamans with Windfury Totem [spell=8512] and Paladins with Blessing of Might [spell=19740] are strong additions.

### Spell Cleave Dungeon Leveling
Spell Cleave groups are built around Mages, who use Blizzard [spell=10] with Improved Blizzard [spell=11185] to control large groups of enemies while dealing the best AoE damage in the game. Warlocks with Rain of Fire [spell=5740] and Hunters with Volley [spell=1510] can add to the damage. It is high risk and high reward, and a well-run group can earn the most experience per hour in the game.
