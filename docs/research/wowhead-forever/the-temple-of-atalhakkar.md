## Guide text
Source: https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide=35200

The Temple of Atal'Hakkar [zone=1477] is located in the Swamp of Sorrows. This guide provides tips and strategies for completing Temple of Atal'Hakkar, including everything you need before and during the dungeon, quests to complete inside, boss strategies, loot, and more.

If you are new to WoW: Forever, please look out for this symbol throughout the guide for additional, beginner-friendly tips.

## Temple of Atal'Hakkar Overview in World of Warcraft: Forever

The recommended level for Temple of Atal'Hakkar is between 50 and 55. In-game players usually refer to Temple of Atal'Hakkar as "Sunken Temple." 

## Location of Temple of Atal'Hakkar in World of Warcraft: Forever

Partially submerged in a large lake in the center of the Swamp of Sorrows [zone=8], the Temple of Atal'Hakkar is pretty easy to access for both factions.Alliance can travel from Nethergarde Keep while Horde can fly to Stonard. Swim across the lake to the temple, and you’ll find the entrance is facing to the south, where you climb stairs to enter. 

Temple of Atal'Hakkar
Stonard
To Nethergarde Keep

Getting to the instance portal is an adventure on its own. Groups should gather in the Swamp of Sorrows [zone=8] and enter the temple together. 

Murk Slitherer [npc=5224] and Fungal Ooze [npc=5235] are present but don’t pose much of a threat. Fight your way through to the Hall of Masks, then, if you’d like to start gathering Fetish of Hakkar [item=6181] and Atal'ai Tablet [item=6288] for your quests, take the right hallway down the stairs to the Chamber of Blood.

When finished in the Chamber of Blood, return up the stairs to return to the Hall of Masks. To your left is The Butchery where a rare mob, Veyzhak the Cannibal [npc=5399] spawns. Check to see if he is spawned. If not, proceed down the hallway until you get to the Den of the Caller. Here, you’ll find humanoid priests that can heal and flee when they get low on health. Be sure to interrupt and prevent them from running into other mob packs.

Take the stairs to the south to reach the upper level of the Chamber of Blood where Kazkaz the Unholy [npc=5401] spawns. There are no quests associated with him, and he shares loot with the regular trash mobs in the area. Once he is defeated, take the stairs back down to the Den of the Caller and head down the eastern hallway.

You’ll enter the Hall of Ritual where several groups spawn very close to one another. Be careful to pull each group one at a time and prevent them from fleeing at low health. Head down the stairs to the Hall of Bones.

You can either now jump down to the middle level or take the stairs on the right side of the room to gain access to the instance entrance.

As with many World of Warcraft: Forever Dungeons, wiping (the entire group dying) in Temple of Atal'Hakkar can be a frustrating time. 

Ideally, if the entire group is not dead, you should be able to get a rez (resurrection). This is a spell someone casts on you to bring your character back to life with a small amount of health and mana. Priests and Shamans learn this early; Paladins need to complete a quest to learn resurrection and may not have it. 

If you can't get a rez, click to release your spirit, then run back to the dungeon from the Spirit Healer as a ghost. You collect your body when you enter the instance.

## Temple of Atal'Hakkar Bosses in World of Warcraft: Forever

Atal'alarion [npc=8580] is a level 50 elite Sandury Troll located in the Pit of Refuse.

Pit of Refuse Shrines
You’ll want to start your journey out by traveling down to the Pit of Refuse. I recommend taking the path to your right when you first walk in (not the stairs). You'll see troll mobs along the way.

Clear all of the trash as you travel around the circular platform. You’ll notice balconies that dip out and overlook the center of the pit. Essentially, once it’s all cleared, each of your party members should spread out and be assigned to a balcony with an Atal’ai Shrine. The shrines must be clicked in a specific order:

- South (Bottom)
- North (Top)
- Southwest (Bottom Left)
- Southeast (Bottom Right)
- Northwest (Top Left)
- Northeast (Top Right)

Once a statue has been clicked in the correct sequence, it’ll turn green. If not, the person attempting to activate will gain a curse. Take turns clicking the Shrines until they all turn green. Then, Atal'alarion [npc=8580] will spawn in the center:

 | 

Atal’alrion Abilities

- Sweeping Slam [spell=12887]
- AoE knockdown

Atal’alrion Strategy
Atal'alarion [npc=8580] has a lot of HP, hits pretty hard, and has an aggro clearing ability. He knocks players high into the air, who then take fall damage. Healers, be prepared to heal your party members when they land. Have the tank drag Atal'alarion [npc=8580] close to a wall, keeping healers and ranged at max distance. Tanks, be prepared to kite Atal'alarion [npc=8580] if your healer get's knocked into the air. Other than taking those abilities into consideration, you should be able to kill him pretty easily.

Once you clear the Pit of Refuse, and kill Atal'alarion [npc=8580] head on back up to the instance portal. I recommend taking the passageway that is second from the left, where you’ll find many living and undead Atal’ai. Head up the stairs, and you’ll reach a landing that looks somewhat similar to where you activated the shrines. Instead, of activating 6 shrines, you’ll have to kill six mini-bosses, ranging from 50-52 elite that unlocks your way to Jammal'an the Prophet [npc=5710]. Each mini-boss is on its own balcony. 

You should run into Loro [npc=5714] first. Essentially just follow your way around the circle, cleaning trash and all of the mini bosses. If you move in a clockwise order, you’ll kill: Gasher [npc=5713], Mijan [npc=5717], Zolo [npc=5712], Zul'Lor [npc=5716] and Hukku [npc=5715]. 

Stairs on the inner circle will lead you to Gasher [npc=5713] and Zul'Lor [npc=5716] while outside stairs lead you to the other four. The Atal'ai Defenders are not particularly challenging and can die pretty easily if your group is prepared.

The Pit of Sacrifice
You’ll now encounter many groups of Dragonkin as you make your way to the Pit of Sacrifice. Navigate around the room, killing the groups one at a time. Be careful not to fall through the hole in the center!

Lair of the Chosen
Head into the east passageway into the Lair of the Chosen. There’s a lot of mixed mobs of both melee & casters, so be extremely careful with your pulls here and watch out for runners. Be careful of the Atal'ai Deathwalker [npc=5271], as they can cast Fear [spell=5782]. When some of the mobs die, ghosts will spawn that focus a player and chase them. Simply kite these ghosts until they despawn. Otherwise, they'll hit you for a lot of damage!

Clear the entire hallway and upcoming room, and now it’s time for Jammal'an the Prophet [npc=5710], a level 54 elite and his assistant, Ogom the Wretched [npc=5711], a level 53 elite.

Jamal'an and Ogom Strategy
Ogom the Wretched [npc=5711] has much less health, should be separated from Jammal'an the Prophet [npc=5710] and killed quickly. Spread out your party as much as possible for when Jammal'an the Prophet [npc=5710] casts Hex of Jammal'an [npc=8505] as it transforms a party member into a hostile Atal'ai Berserker. Avoid getting hit by the Berserker, and nuke down Jammal'an the Prophet [npc=5710] after Hex of Jammal'an [npc=8505] falls.

After Jammal'an the Prophet [npc=5710] has fallen, the two drakes Weaver [npc=5720] & Dreamscythe [npc=5721] will spawn in the Pit of Sacrifice.

Weaver & Dreamscythe Strategy

They’re relatively easy to burn down. Tanks should tank with their backs to a wall, keeping the drakes facing away from the party to avoid their frontal cone and knockback abilities.

After killing Weaver [npc=5720] & Dreamscythe [npc=5721], two more drakes, Hazzas [npc=5722] & Morphaz [npc=5719], will spawn in the hallway right outside the Chamber of the Dreamer.

Hazzas & Morphaz's Abilities

- Acid Breath [spell=12533]
- Wing Flap [spell=12882]

Hazzas & Morphaz Strategy

Fighting Hazzas and Morphaz is the same strategy as the previous two drakes. Try not to pull both at the same time and avoid any trash in the area. These are the last two big kills before Shade of Eranikus [npc=5709].

Shade of Eranikus [npc=5709], a level 55 elite, will summon any leftover dragonkin in the instance to his side, so it’s important to make sure you’ve cleared them all before pulling the boss.

The Shade of Eranikus' Abilities

- War Stomp [spell=16740]
- Deep Slumber [spell=12890]
- Thrash [spell=3391]
- Acid Breath [spell=12533]

The Shade of Eranikus' Strategy

Ranged, stay out of melee range to avoid his AoE stun ability, War Stomp [spell=16740]. The target with the highest threat will get afflicted with Deep Slumber [spell=12890]. Players can use anti-aggro abilities such as Fade [spell=10941],  [spell=1038] and Feint [spell=11303] to help control who gets afflicted with Deep Slumber [spell=12890]. Melee, never stand in front of the boss and be wary of his War Stomp [spell=16740] ability. DPS him down following the mechanics and you’ll be fine. It’s a long fight so healers, be sure to keep an eye on your mana pool!

Head to the west side of the Pit of Sacrifice and you’ll enter the Sanctum of the Fallen God. Clear the trash along the way and to prepare for the encounter. Avatar of Hakkar Strategy

Kill the adds until a Hakkari Bloodkeeper [npc=8438] spawns. Kill it and grab the Hakkari Blood [item=10460] from the corpse. Groups usually keep Free-for-All loot on in this encounter so one designated person can loot the bloods. (Don't forget to switch it back after Hakkar spawns!)

Using the blood, you can extinguish one of the four braziers in the room. Proceed until another Hakkari Bloodkeeper [npc=8438] spawns, and all of the braziers are extinguished.

If everyone moves close to a wall before extinguishing the final brazier, you can drop combat before engaging the Avatar of Hakkar [npc=8443]. The fight itself is pretty straightforward. He'll cast Cause Insanity [spell=12888] on a random player. The best strategy is to stun or CC that player until they're returned to normal. If possible, Dispel  [spell=10896], too. Otherwise, DPS him down and prepare for victory!
