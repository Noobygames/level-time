 The Cozy Sleeping Bag quest chain will lead you all over Azeroth, giving generous amounts of experience at each step, coupled with Studen Fodder to help you make up for time on the trail. You need to be Level 14 to start this Quest Line What is a Cozy Sleeping Bag? The Cozy Sleeping Bag was first introduced in Season of Discovery. It is an item that puts down a Sleeping Bag; when you lie down in it, you get a buff to experience of 1% for every minute lying down, up to a maximum of 3. The buff will last for one hour. While inside your cozy sleeping bag, you also become rested. Alongside this, you will also be rewarded Student Fodder x 5 throughout the chain. In WoW Forever this regenerates your health.  Comment by Rihhiharti 1. The Absolute #1 Horde Gotcha: The Starting Zone Lock Do NOT follow the Alliance-centric guides that tell you to run to Westfall first. If you are Horde, the starting order is strictly continent-locked. • The Rule: You MUST interact with the Burned-Out Remains in The Barrens (/way 46.7, 74.3) south of Camp Taurajo first to acquire the item "...and that note you found". • The Penalty: If you try to sneak into Westfall first, the starting triggers at the Alexston Farmstead wreckage simply will not phase or appear for you. Save yourself a boat ride and start in Kalimdor. 2. Low-Level Survival Routing: Grom'gol to Westfall If you are attempting this chain at level 14, do not walk through Duskwood. The mob density and high aggro radius on the roads near Darkshire mean instant death for a low-level toon. • The Safer Route: Take the Zeppelin to Grom'gol Base Camp in Stranglethorn Vale. Instead of heading north on the road, run straight west into the ocean. Swim north along the western coastline of Stranglethorn Vale until you hit the southern shores of Westfall. This completely bypasses the high-level mobs in both STV and Duskwood, dropping you safely right next to the Westfall farmlands. 3. The Graveyard "Snap-Back" Anti-Exploit Warning If you try to use the Arathi Highlands border death shortcut to ghost-walk all the way down through the Wetlands and the Dun Algaz tunnels into Loch Modan, be warned: The game will block you from resurrecting at the Loch Modan Spirit Healer if your corpse is too far away. • The Mechanic: Clicking "Accept" at the Loch Modan healer while your body is at the Arathi gate will forcefully snap your living character all the way back to the Arathi Highlands graveyard. • The Workaround: Run alive through the Wetlands road as far as you can. If the Alliance Mountaineers or Dragonmaw Orcs kill you inside or right outside the Dun Algaz tunnels, that death puts your corpse close enough to Loch Modan to successfully break the anchor, allowing you to take resurrection sickness in Loch Modan safely. 4. Final Recommendation: Is Level 14 Worth It? While the item itself requires Level 14 to start, the sheer level of zone scaling (dealing with level 20–25 elite zone guards and high-level mobs) makes it an absolute gauntlet for a fresh toon. • The Verdict: Unless you have a high-level friend escorting you or you enjoy the pure adrenaline of tactical deaths, wait until level 20 to 25 before attempting this chain on a Horde alt. At that point, your aggro radius is manageable, your gear won't completely break from random road mobs, and you will still get massive mileage out of the stacking 3% XP boost and the 5x Student Fodder items for the remainder of your leveling journey.

 Camping is a new system introduced in World of Warcraft: Forever, providing brilliant buffs as well as the chance to rest and be social. Camping Overview in WoW Forever Camping is a new crafting system; it isn't quite a profession. Instead, it sits alongside your professions and adds extra depth. If you played Vanilla or Classic WoW, you may remember crafting a Campfire and receiving a small Spirit Buff, Cozy Fire . Camping in Forever is the next step from this. Setting down a camp creates a small campsite that other passing players can visit. Campsite visitors can share crafted items that provide buffs, vendors, tradeskill spaces, and more. You can begin interacting with the Campsite system from early on in your Forever leveling journey; in fact, with all the available buffs, it will likely be recommended that you do. Shown at the Forever Deep Dive at BlizzCon We don't yet know how far this system will go; everything seen so far, though, shows it as infinitely expandable and suggests that players of all levels will be interacting with this for a long time to come.. All Perks and Benefits of a Campsite in Forever At either your own Campsite or another player's, you can add objects that will give you helpful buffs, add vendors that can potentially sell useful items, repair and more, or add improved tradeskill items that allow crafting on the move. The Great Outdoors You will be sent to the nearest town and told to train cooking to learn to craft your own Basic Campfire. You can craft your campfire right away; the items you need: Simple Wood , and Flint and Tinder are sold by the Cooking Trainer. At this stage, with your Basic Campfire, you will be able to have 3 items placed in your camp (note: you can only place one yourself; you need to find friends for the other two). You will be able to upgrade your camp, though, to enable more items later on. At this point, you will also be given a 101 Quest for every profession you have trained. You hand this in to your Profession Trainer and will be taught the Tier One Item relating to that profession. Shown at the Forever Deep Dive at BlizzCon Profession Items and Buffs from Camping in WoW Forever Every Profession has access to Items that can be crafted and placed in camps. Each item has three tiers, with a stronger effect each tier. You can trade your items but you need to have a particular skill in the profession in order to place the item. Anyone can receive the Camp Item benefit, though; that is not bound to a profession.  Scarlet Banner is obtained in WoW: Forever by solving a puzzle that spans four zones and ends in the Scarlet Monastery Library. Players collect four Echoes from graves, then activate them at the correct statues to make the banner appear. Right now, it appears that the Scarlet Banner is simply a cosmetic item, though that might change in the future. If this puzzle looks familiar to you then it probably is! This originally appeared in Season of Discovery as the Pain Suppression Rune . Scarlet Banner Quick Facts in WoW: Forever Reward: Scarlet Banner Recommended Level: 28+ to Enter Scarlet Monastery Zones Visited: Scarlet Monastery, Arathi Highlands, Dustwallow Marsh, Swamp of Sorrows Final Step: Scarlet Monastery Library wing Scarlet Banner Echo Locations in WoW: Forever Each Echo is looted from a grave. Collect all four before heading to the Library. Scarlet Monastery Graveyard Echo Graveyard Echo is about halfway down the grave area in the Scarlet Monastery Graveyard wing, to the left inside a fenced area. Arathi Highlands Echo Arathi Echo is at Go'shek Farm in Arathi Highlands, at /way 62.0 54.0. Echo grave Dustwallow Marsh Echo Theramore Echo is looted from Grave in Dustwallow Marsh, at /way 63.7 42.5. Echo grave Swamp of Sorrows Echo Swamp Echo is in Swamp of Sorrows, at /way 16.8 53.8. Echo grave Scarlet Monastery Library Statue Order in WoW: Forever With all four Echoes in your bags, enter the Scarlet Monastery Library wing and activate each Echo at its statue in this order: Warrior Statue: Swamp Echo Mage Statue: Arathi Echo Paladin Statue: Theramore Echo Priest Statue: Graveyard Echo If each stage has been completed correctly, a glowing object appears in the middle of the room. Loot it to receive Scarlet Banner .
