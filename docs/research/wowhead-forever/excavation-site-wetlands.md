#### Shadetooth (NPC ids [260325])
Overview: Shadetooth is a very unforgiving encounter, particularly when precautions aren't taken. Shadetooth is joined by two Thicket Hunters, and fighting all 3 targets at the same time is very difficult unless you're overleveled. The Thicket Hunters will use Infected Wound on their current target. This should be dispelled as quickly as possible. The mobs can be crowd-controlled (CC), and I would highly recommend either keeping the adds CC'd for the entire fight as you kill Shadetooth, or CC one and focus the other before killing the second one. When there's only Shadetooth left, there are a few things you have to deal with. First is Rend, which deals moderate damage to the tank. The second mechanic is Terrifying Roar, a 5-second fear that cannot be dispelled. On its own, this is not too scary, but there are raptors lurking in the bushes. It is very easy to get feared into additional trash mobs by spawning raptors through the grass or adds that haven't been cleared. To make this fight as easy as possible, clear out the crocodile cove to the left and use that area to fight the boss. The Thicket Hunters can't swim, so using the previous boss area to fight the boss will reset the boss. It's worth noting that the boss can be slowed and stunned, which is particularly useful during his Enrage once he reaches 30%.
Role notes: tank: Clear out the crocodile cove to the left and use that area to fight the boss.; healer: Keep your distance from the boss to avoid being feared.; damage: CC or kill the Thicket Hunters before killing the boss. Ranged DPS should keep their distance from the boss to avoid being feared.

## Guide text
Source: https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide=34967

Excavation Site: Wetlands [zone=16732] is located in the Wetlands, Eastern Kingdoms. This guide provides tips and strategies for completing Excavation Site, including everything you need before and during the dungeon, quests to complete inside, boss strategies, loot, and more.

If you are new to WoW: Forever, please look out for this symbol throughout the guide for additional, beginner-friendly tips.

## Excavation Site Overview in World of Warcraft: Forever

 The Explorer's League, a band of archeologists researching the origins of the Dwarven race, has long had a presence in the mountainous regions of the Wetlands with Whelgar's Excavation Site, a camp overrun with raptors, serving as its main hub. Strange activity was noted to the southeast of the site, and a new camp was set up to explore the newly uncovered titan activity. 

The recommended level for Excavation Site is between 26 and 32. Even if you can pick up the quests at 24, entering the dungeon at that point is ill-advised, as the last boss is level 31. 

## Location of Excavation Site in World of Warcraft: Forever

Excavation Site is located in Wetlands [zone=11] and is a new installment in WoW: Forever. In previous Classic iterations, this area was inaccessible. But with newly constructed roads, you can now find the entrance to the new dungeon at the top of the hill. 

Entrance to Excavation Site
Meeting Stone and Entry

Alliance can easily access the dungeon from Menethil Harbor, which now has a boat connection with Stormwind through Auberdine, or you can run on foot through Loch Modan. Most players will have started their questing journey in the Wetlands at this point and will likely have a flight path in the zone already. 

Horde will have a slightly longer trek, as they have to go through Hillsbrad Foothills and Arathi Highlands on foot to reach Wetlands. Make sure to pick up flight paths in Tarren Mill and Hammerfall during your walk to make your future experience slightly more pleasant. 

As with many World of Warcraft: Forever Dungeons, wiping (the entire group dying) in Excavation Site can be a frustrating time due to the long run-back. Any player who has made the infamous run from Teldrassil to Ironforge will know this graveyard very well, as it's the only one in the zone. If your group fully wipes, you'll have to run across half the Wetlands map before reentering the dungeon. 

Ideally, if the entire group is not dead, you should be able to get a rez (resurrection). This is a spell someone casts on you to bring your character back to life with a small amount of health and mana. Priests, Druids, and Shamans learn this early; Paladins need to complete a quest to learn resurrection and may not have it.

If you can't get a rez, click to release your spirit, then run back to the dungeon from the Spirit Healer (marked on the map below) as a ghost. You collect your body when you enter the instance.

## Noteworthy Excavation Site Trash Mobs in World of Warcraft: Forever

The most dangerous section of the dungeon by far is the trash area of Saltspine [npc=260322], the second boss. If you've ever played Pokémon, you will know that walking in tall grass may cause you to engage in an unwanted encounter, and this dungeon is no different. Lurking in the grass, you'll find Thicket Lurker [npc=260800]. This is a unique type of mob, because they are not in stealth in the bushes, waiting to strike. No, they quite literally function like Pokémons in tall grass, spawning if you walk around in it long enough. 

As if this wasn't enough, Thicket Matriarch [npc=260802] will be spread around the area, and even if you avoid gathering the attention of the Lurkers, once the Matriarch reaches low health, they'll screech and call Lurkers to their aid. 

Strategy: Stay out of the grass and traverse the narrow path. Try to fight in more open areas to avoid accidentally stepping into the grass while already fighting other mobs. If you're fighting Matriarchs with other mobs, make sure to nuke down the non-Matriarch first, so that you'll have as few mobs present as possible when the screech summons the Lurkers.
