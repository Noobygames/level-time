 Welcome to Wowhead's guide to the class quests for the Temple of Atal'Hakkar (Sunken Temple) in WoW Forever. Each class has its own quest chain that sends you into The Temple of Atal'Hakkar , and each one ends with three rewards. Below is a complete walkthrough for every class, with maps for each location. These quests become available at level 50 from your Class Trainer, though realistically you will probably want to wait until you are a little bit higher level because some can be quite hard. Druid Sunken Temple Quest Chain in WoW: Forever Step 1: Find Torwa Pathfinder As a Druid, your quest begins at any of your class trainers in any major city. Pick up Torwa Pathfinder from your trainer and head to Un'Goro Crater to speak with Torwa Pathfinder . /way Un'Goro Crater 71, 76 Torwa Pathfinder Torwa Pathfinder Step 2: Bloodpetal Ingredients Torwa will give you Bloodpetal Poison , which requires you to bring him 8x Gorishi Sting and 8x Bloodcap . Gorishi Sting can be looted from Gorishi mobs at their hive in the south of Un'Goro Crater. These have around a 10% drop rate. The Bloodcap s can be looted from Bloodpetal Sprout s, which are small plants located all around Un'Goro Crater. /way Un'Goro Crater 50, 76 Gorishi Hive Gorishi Mobs Torwa Pathfinder Step 3: Test the Toxin on a Devilsaur When you have all the items, return to Torwa and take his next quest, Toxic Test . This gives you the item Devilsaur Barb and requires you to use it on a Devilsaur . Any of the 3 Devilsaur varieties will work for this. They are generally to the west in Un'Goro Crater. A good strategy to do this safely is to Soothe Animal then Hibernate the Devilsaur, and then stab it with the Toxic Horn. Step 4: Defeat Atal'alarion When you have done this task, head back to Torwa to get the last part of this quest series, A Better Ingredient . This sends you into the Sunken Temple to kill Atal'alarion and loot Putrid Vine from him. Find or form a group and make your way into the Sunken Temple. When you have retrieved this item, head back to Torwa to hand in your quest and be rewarded with Grizzled Pelt , Forest's Embrace and Moonshadow Stave . Hunter Sunken Temple Quest Chain in WoW: Forever Step 1: Find Ogtinc As a Hunter, your Sunken Temple quest chain begins with your class trainer in any major city. Pick up The Hunter's Charm from your trainer and head to Azshara to speak with Ogtinc . He is located at the edge of the cliff that drops off onto the beach. /way Azshara 42.4, 42.6 Ogtinc Ogtinc Step 2: Perfect Courser Antlers Hand in the quest and pick up the next one he offers, Courser Antlers . This requires you to get 2x Perfect Courser Antler , which can be looted from Mosshoof Courser . These have a 10% drop rate, so it may take some time just to gather 2. You will find Mosshoof Coursers in almost all of Azshara, except for the ruins and the beach where the Naga are located. A good way to farm these is to run from Ogtinc all the way east to the edge of the cliff and then back to him, killing the Mosshoof Coursers along the way. Step 3: Wavethrasher Scales Once you have gathered 2x Perfect Courser Antler, head back to Ogtinc and pick up the next quest, Wavethrashing , which requires you to bring him 6x Wavethrasher Scales . These can be looted from Young Wavethrasher , Wavethrasher and Great Wavethrasher . You will find these mobs around the coast to the far north and southeast. The drop rate on these items is around 30%, so with any luck it shouldn't take too long. /way Azshara 86, 12 Wavethrashers /way Azshara 88, 32 Wavethrashers /way Azshara 79, 84 Wavethrashers Wavethrashers Wavethrashers Wavethrashers Ogtinc Step 4: Defeat Morphaz When you have your 6x Wavethrasher Scale, head back to Ogtinc again to pick up the final part of this series, The Green Drake . This sends you into the Sunken Temple to kill Morphaz and loot Tooth of Morphaz from him. Find or form a group and make your way into the Sunken Temple. When you have retrieved this item, head back to Ogtinc to hand in your quest and be rewarded with Hunting Spear , Devilsaur Eye and Devilsaur Tooth . Mage Sunken Temple Quest Chain in WoW: Forever Step 1: Find Archmage Xylem As a Mage, your Sunken Temple quest chain begins with your class trainer in any major city. Pick up Magecraft , which sends you to Sanath Lim-yo in Azshara . He will end the quest but not give you a new one. To get the next part, take his teleport and head up to Archmage Xylem , who will give you Magic Dust . /way Azshara 28, 50 Sanath Lim-yo /way Azshara 29, 40 Archmage Xylem Sanath Lim-yo Archmage Xylem Step 2: Collect Magic Dust from Blood Elves This quest requires you to loot 10x Glittering Dust , which can be looted from Blood Elf Surveyor and Blood Elf Reclaimer . They can be found to the east of Xylem, as marked on the map. /way Azshara 55, 28 Blood Elves Blood Elves Archmage Xylem Step 3: Spitelash Sirens When you have 10x Magic Dust, return to Xylem and pick up the next quest, The Siren's Coral , which requires you to loot 6x Enchanted Coral from Spitelash Siren . These can be found all throughout the Ruins of Eldarath and the Bay of Storms, southeast of Xylem and south of the Blood Elf mobs you killed in the previous quest. These have around an 80% drop rate, so it shouldn't take too many kills to finish this one up. Step 4: Defeat Morphaz When you have them, return to Xylem to pick up the last quest of this series, Destroy Morphaz , which sends you into the Sunken Temple to kill Morphaz and loot Arcane Shard from him. Find or form a group and make your way into the Sunken Temple. When you have retrieved this item, head back to Xylem to hand in your quest and be rewarded with Glacial Spike , Arcane Crystal Pendant and Fire Ruby . Shaman Sunken Temple Quest Chain in WoW: Forever Step 1: Gather the Elementals As a Shaman, your quest begins at any of your class trainers in any major city. Pick up Elemental Mastery , which requires you to get 1 of each: Elemental Air , Elemental Fire , Elemental Earth , and Elemental Water . You can buy these off the Auction House, which shouldn't cost much, apart from the Elemental Earth maybe being a few gold. If you can't afford them or want to farm them yourself, they drop from elemental mobs of each respective elemental type. Step 2: Find Bath'rah the Windwatcher When you have these, head to Bath'rah the Windwatcher . He can be found in Alterac Mountains and will give you the follow-up quest Spirit Totem , which requires you to bring him 8x Bloodshot Spider Eye and 8x Thick Black Claw . /way Alterac Mountains 80, 66 Bath'rah the Windwatcher Bath'rah the Windwatcher Step 3: Spider Eyes and Black Claws Bloodshot Spider Eyes can be looted from Carrion Vulture and Venom Mist Lurker . Thick Black Claws can be looted from Carrion Vulture and Diseased Black Bear . All of these mobs can be found at the entrance to Western Plaguelands from Tirisfal Glades . These have around a 20% drop rate, so it shouldn't take you too long to gather them up. /way Western Plaguelands 32, 62 Spider Eye and Black Claw mobs Spider Eye and Black Claw mobs Step 4: Collect the Voodoo Feathers When you have them, return to Bath'rah, and he will give you Da Voodoo . This requires you to go into the Sunken Temple and loot 2 of each Voodoo Feather from the troll minibosses. See the Voodoo Feathers table above for which boss drops each one. Find or make a group, run the Sunken Temple, and collect these items. Once you are finished, head back to Bath'rah and complete the final quest of this chain. You will be rewarded with Azurite Fists , Wildstaff , and Enamored Water Spirit .
