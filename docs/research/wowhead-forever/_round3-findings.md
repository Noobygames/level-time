## Facts per topic (source in the named file)
- Dungeon quests (LT dungeon quest tickets): `dungeon-quests-all.md` (guide 34916) lists every dungeon quest with min level, side, effort, shareable, giver location, prerequisites, plus per-dungeon recommended levels (Hard/Medium/At Level/Easy) and required keys. Its own comments list errors (Heart of Disruption giver is in Hillsbrad/Tarren Mill; Searching for the Lost Satchel also from Rahauro; City of Dalaran and The Coldbringer's Eye missing). Level column = quest minimum level, not dungeon level.
- Dungeon level ranges differ by source: dungeon guide 34783 says Ruins of Lordaeron 15-20, Excavation 24-29, Dalaran 28-33; roadmap/overview guides 34785/34781 say Ruins 16-22, Excavation 26-32, Dalaran 28-34. Check which one dungeon_db uses; mark unverified.
- Release/roadmap (`roadmap-overview.md`): launch 2026-11-04 (level cap 60); Barrow Deeps (10-player), Hyjal Summit (20-player) and Onyxia (40-player) on 2026-12-09; Hardcore mode winter 2026; later: 2 new raids + 2 new dungeons (spring 2027). Four new zones: Mount Hyjal (60), Shen'Dralas, Riverglades (35-45), Zephras Isle (1-12, Skyborne). Beta cap was level 30.
- Transport (`transport-flight-points.md`, guide 35352): flight masters per faction incl. Riverglades (Farholde Keep) and Hyjal; new ship routes Steamwheedle Port <-> Powderfuse Port (Riverglades) and Dalaran <-> Valanaar Skydocks (Zephras Isle); ships/zeppelins every 3-5 min; "Request Stop" button on flights.
- Sleeping bag / camping (`sleeping-bag-camping.md`, LT-125): Cozy Sleeping Bag quest line needs level 14; lying in bag = +1% XP per minute up to 3%, buff lasts 1 hour, bag also gives rested state and regenerates health in Forever; campsite system (profession perks, leatherworking Camp Tent = rested XP up to 5% of a level).
- Systems (`systems-damage-meter-edit-mode.md`): Forever has a built-in Damage Meter (damage, DPS, taken, avoidable taken, interrupts, healing, deaths; multiple meters, Edit Mode), Ping system, Edit Mode with layouts, Legacy system (account-wide points, challenges per class at 25/45/60). Relevant for run recap overlap: check `C_DamageMeter` in `make api-docs` (forever).
- Sunken Temple class quests (guide 35364), reputations Shen'dralar/Timbermaw/Gelkis/Magram, Onyxia attunement (guide 35334), library books (Dire Maul), toys from Alcaz/BRD: see `sunken-temple-class-quests.md`, `reputations-dungeon-related.md`, `collectibles-misc.md`.
- No guide for Blackmaw Hold / Shaper's Terrace; Barrow Deeps / Hyjal Summit raid pages say "Coming Soon" (guide 34784).
