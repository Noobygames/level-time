 The customizable in-game Damage Meter in World of Warcraft: Forever displays your performance and the performance of teammates at a glance. Track Damage Done, Avoidable Damage Taken, Interrupts, Healing, and more. Edit Mode The Damage Meter The Ping System Discord Integration What is the Damage Meter in World of Warcraft: Forever The Damage Meter is a UI element that shows group performance at a glance. It can be set to display various data including Damage Done, DPS, HPS, utility used, deaths, and more. You can position the Damage Meter anywhere on your screen using Edit Mode , so that it will be easy to check quickly in the heat of combat if need be. While you can easily click to see tracked data, if you want to be able to see multiple data sets at a time you can create multiple Damage Meters and set each to show what matters to you. Using the Damage Meter you can easily see how much damage you are doing on a fight and how much you are doing relative to your team mates. You can see who is getting interrupts. You can see how each member of your party died. While it is not necessarily a good measure of effectiveness, you can see your party's heals per second which can give you a good view of the healing needs for a fight. And this isn't all. This very powerful little tool is excellent for raid and team leaders, as well as anyone looking to better their play. What Can the Damage Meter Do in World of Warcraft: Forever? Don't let the name fool you; Damage Dealt isn't the only metric available from the Damage Meter. The Damage Meter keeps track of data for three categories of play: Damage, Healing, and Actions. To select the category and detail that you want to display, click the dropdown arrow at the left of the Damage Meter header bar. Damage Done : The total amount of damage each party member did to enemy targets. The number in parentheses is that player's DPS. DPS : The total damage per second each party member did to enemy targets. The number in parentheses is that player's Damage Done. Damage Taken : The total amount of damage taken by each party member. Avoidable Damage Taken : The total amount of avoidable damage taken by each party member. You really want to be the lowest on this metric! Enemy Damage Taken : The total amount of damage each enemy took. Healing Done : The total amount of healing each party member did to friendly targets. The number in parentheses is that player's HPS. HPS : The total healing per second each party member did to friendly targets. The number in parentheses is that player's Healing Done These settings are counts of the number of times each player performed these actions: How many times each player got off a successful interrupt. Note that this only includes hard interrupts, not stuns, displacements, or other abilities that may also result in an interrupt. How many times each player got off a successful dispel. How many times each player died. You can click on each bar for even more details. How Do I Enable the Damage Meter in World of Warcraft: Forever? There are two steps for getting the Damage Meter set up. You will need to enable it in your settings , and then position it in Edit Mode . Enabling the Damage Meter Open your game menu (default Esc ) and click Options Select Advanced Options from the left menu Scroll down to the Damage Meter header Select the Enable Damage Meter check box You can also select Auto Reset Damage Meter if you don't want to have to clear it manually every time you enter an instance Moving and Customizing the Damage Meter Open your game menu (default Esc ) and click Edit Mode Click on the Damage Meter to open the Edit Mode sub-menu This allows you to edit different elements of the Damage Meter, including bar sizes, text size, padding between bars, and more Move the Damage Meter by clicking and dragging it How Do I Set Up the Damage Meter in World of Warcraft: Forever? The Damage Meter is a very powerful tool and setting up exactly what you want to see will help you analyze your gameplay and see where you need to improve. Metrics To set up the metrics that you want to see, click the dropdown arrow at the left of the Damage Meter header bar. Hovering over each category allows you to select the detail that you want to display. Settings To access Settings, click the cogwheel button at the right of the Damage Meter header bar. This dropdown allows you to access several functions. Settings and Edit Mode are shortcuts to the Damage Meter sections in your Options menu and Edit Mode . Make Noniteractive is a toggle that prevents you from inadvertently making changes to your Damage Meter during gameplay. Reset Data and Delete Window allow you to manually reset the data saved in your Damage Meter and to remove an additional Damage Meter window if you created one. Create New Window allows you to create additional Damage Meters to show other metrics at a glance. Segment Data Clicking the O button to the left of the setting cogwheel allows you to change the Damage Meter to display Current Segment or Overall data. Overall data displays information for the entire instance, while the Current Segment option lists each segment where a player entered and left combat.

 Edit Mode The Damage Meter The Ping System Discord Integration What is the Ping System in World of Warcraft: Forever? The Radial Wheel Interface The Ping System is a quick and easy marker system that allows you to draw attention to a location or object in game. More information can be imparted by using any of four additional ping markers. These markers can be placed, or pinged , using keybindings, a radial wheel interface, or macros. A ping can also appear as chat lines for additional attention and for Text-to-Speech. Advanced uses for the Ping System include communication to your group about your actions or status through the use of keybindings and macros. How Do I Set Up the Ping System in World of Warcraft: Forever? There are a few steps needed to set up the Ping system. You will need to enable it in your settings , and then set keybindings . Enabling the Ping system Open your game menu (default Esc ) and click Options Select Ping System from the left menu Select the Enable Pings check box There are a few more settings that you can use to refine your experience, although they are not required for basic use Setting Keybindings Click the Keybindings button. If you have already closed your Options menu you can access Keybindings as follows: Open your game menu (default Esc ) and click Options Select Keybindings from the left menu Open the Ping System menu Set a Keybinding for Ping You can also set Keybindings for the additional Ping types, although that is not required Once you have enabled the Ping System and set your keybind you are ready to use the Ping system. How Do I Use the Ping System in World of Warcraft: Forever? Using the Ping system is easy; move your mouse to where you want to Ping, and press your Ping Keybind. A Ping marker will briefly show at your selected location. Default Ping If you move your mouse to where you want to Ping, and hold down your Ping Keybind, the Radial Wheel Interface will open allowing you to select additional Ping types. Move your mouse to the type you want, and release your Ping Keybind. That Ping marker will briefly show at your selected location, the center of the Radial Wheel. If you ping too many times in close succession, you will incur a brief cooldown before being able to Ping again. Assist Ping Attack Ping Warning Ping On My Way Ping While each Ping icon has an assigned message, many players will not have memorized what they mean so they may just be interpreted as different color Pings, which can also be helpful. Now that you have the basics down, let's explore more options. The Ping System setup in your Options menu has several other useful settings. Ping Mode Ping Mode has two settings, Quick Ping and Relaxed Ping . Relaxed Mode leaves your Pings visible for a bit longer than Quick Ping. Note that the Radial Wheel does not work with Relaxed Ping. Ping Sounds You can toggle sound on or off for pings. Each type of ping has a slightly different sound. The slider allows you to adjust ping volume. Ping Target Ping Target can be set to Show All , Environment , and Units . Show All (default): Pings will hit both Environmental and Units in the world. Environment : Pings will only hit environmental objects or locations in the world, ignoring all units. This means you will not accidentally ping a player, boss, or other character. Units : Pings will only hit units in the world, ignoring all environmental objects or locations. This means you will only be able to ping players, bosses, or other characters. You can set a keybind for Ping Target which will toggle between Show All and Environment. Pings in Chat View Chat Settings Show Pings in Chat Show Pings in Chat allows just that: your ping will also show in chat. The Chat Settings button opens the same settings window that you can access by right-clicking on your chat tabs. To enable Ping for a chat channel: Select the channel you want Pings to show in from the tabs across the top Select Other from the left menu Check the Ping option near the bottom of the Other section You can also set a color for your Ping notification by clicking the colored box next to the Ping setting Pings on Raid Frames Show Pings on Raid Frames Show Pings on Raid Frames allows players' Pings to appear in their cell on your Raid Frames. This feature is not available for Party Frames. This can be useful for quick communication, eg. signaling that you can get a dangerous mechanic, or letting healers know that you don't have a personal available when targeted by a deadly ability. Advanced Ping System Features in World of Warcraft: Forever The Ping System is quite versatile and has several other useful features Ping Your Resources Instead of pinging the environment, a player, or a target, you can ping various UI elements to trigger useful alerts. Ping your Health Bar to share your current health percentage to the group. Ping your Action Bars or Cooldown Manager spells to share readiness or cooldown information. If you ping a spell or item that is on cooldown, instead of a blue ping showing ready, it will be a green ping showing the remaining cooldown. This function does not work for macros on your Action Bar and may not work with some Action Bar addons. When you ping your own spell or item it makes a unique sound, puts the icon of your spell or item over your head, and leaves a chat message. Ping Macros Resource and basic pings can be put in macros so that they can easily be used from your keyboard. Resource bars can be pinged directly, this does not work with some addons and the macro is a good workaround. You can also use the macro commands within other macros to ping a spell's status every time you use it. Ping macros are not on the GCD. This means that if they are worked into macros along with other commands, they will execute at the same time as those other commands. Because of this, if you use, for example, /pingspell, using your macro will only show the ready status. To get the cooldown status you will have to use it twice. /pingspell and /pingitem : This command can be used in macros to tell your party the status of your spells or items. #showtooltip /pingspell Spell Reflection /use Spell Reflection These work exactly like pinging your Action Bar but work with a keyboard press instead of involving your mouse. /ping @cursor : This command can be used in a macro to just use a basic ping similarly to a Quick Ping. Move your mouse where you want to ping and use your macro. /ping @cursor

 The Heads-Up Display (HUD) Edit Mode allows you to customize your World of Warcraft: Forever User Interface (UI). Learn the basics of using this system to create your perfect UI. Edit Mode The Damage Meter The Ping System Discord Integration How Do I Access Edit Mode in World of Warcraft: Forever? To access Edit Mode just open your Game Menu (default Esc ) and click the Edit Mode button. This opens the main Edit Mode menu, allowing you to change your layouts, adjust settings for UI elements, toggle UI elements on or off, and save or select your custom layouts. To exit Edit Mode, press Esc. From this menu you can select your layout, show and set the size of a background grid if you want one, and toggle snap to elements to make lining your UI up easier. The main menu provides plenty of options for anyone trying to edit the most common elements of your UI. However, those looking to fully customize their layout will want to check the Advanced Options box to see toggles for other important elements like the Stance Bar, Extra Action Button, and more. How Do I Edit My UI Using Edit Mode in World of Warcraft: Forever? While in Edit Mode, all adjustable UI elements are highlighted in blue. The element that you have selected is highlighted in yellow. Here are the basics of editing your UI: Selecting a UI element will bring up a sub-menu with options specific to that element. Each UI element will have different customization options in its sub-menu. UI element sub-menus include quick access to other settings and options they have available, identified by the red buttons at the bottom of the sub-menu. UI elements can be moved by clicking and dragging them anywhere on your screen. Having ‘Snap to Elements’ checked in the main Edit Mode menu will cause the UI element that you’re moving to attach itself to the nearest grid line or UI element. Main menu (left) and UI element sub-menu (right) in Edit Mode Note that if a UI element menu is blocking what you want to look at, you can click and drag it to a different area of your screen. This includes the main Edit Mode menu. How do I Share and Import Layouts in WoW: Forever? When you are happy with your Layout, you can save it by clicking the Layout dropdown bar on the main Edit Mode menu and selecting the New Layout option. Name your Layout and save it. It will now be accessible to all of your characters. Here are some key features of layouts: There are two Preset Layouts available as a base to build off. Saved layouts can be named to keep them organized. Layouts can be made character-specific, meaning they'll only appear as an option for the associated character. Use the 'Share' and 'Import' options to swap layouts with others!

Players will earn Legacy Points for completing challenges, such as leveling different classes and professions. Players can spend these points to gain perks from the Legacy Tree and, at certain milestone Legacy Point totals, receive cosmetic rewards on the Legacy Progress Track. When you earn points, you can spend them within the Legacy Tree. The perks you gain are account-bound, meaning each character you level will have access to these perks. Best Way to Spend Legacy Points in Forever All of the perks have their uses; for example, players who enjoy fishing will want Luremaster and players looking to max their reputations are sure to pick up Diplomat . However, there are some that stand out as being useful in more general situations. Best Legacy Tree Perks When Leveling in Forever The Adventure tree is where you will find perks for leveling. The three stand-out options are: is a good option for reducing the amount of time you need to spend recovering after combat, and generally the best option if you have less than 5 Legacy Points. Well Rested is a nice bonus if you are playing less frequently, or switching between leveling different characters. Also, if you have more than 5 Legacy Points, this is probably the best option, as it is a prerequisite for Talented. Talented is extremely powerful when leveling, allowing you to reach crucial talents up to five levels earlier than you otherwise would have. Best Legacy Tree Perks for Professions in Forever The Professions tree contains quite a few situational talents, so the best option will depend on your professions and levels. If you are leveling your professions, Working Overtime is the best option. This reduces the number of actions needed to level, making it faster and cheaper. Miners, Herbalists, and Skinners will generally want to pick up Bountiful Harvest . Scarce materials are often much more valuable than normal materials, so a higher chance of gathering them significantly increases profits. Dedicated Study is a good option for players looking to max out all of their professions. Often the final levels are particularly difficult and expensive to level, so reaching a high level in your professions and then using this to finish leveling to 300 will save gold. Fastest way to earn Legacy Points While Leveling in Forever If you are looking to earn some Legacy Points while leveling, there are some Legacy Challenges that stand out as the easiest to complete: The Novice, Experienced, and Master challenges for each class reward a Legacy Point at levels 25, 45, and 60.
