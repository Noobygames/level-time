## Guide text
Source: https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide=35224

Dire Maul [zone=2557] is a sprawling dungeon hub located in Feralas. This guide provides tips and strategies for completing the Warpwood Quarter of Dire Maul, including everything you need before and during the dungeon, quests to complete inside, boss strategies, loot, and more.

If you are new to WoW: Forever, please look out for this symbol throughout the guide for additional, beginner-friendly tips.

## Dire Maul Warpwood Quarter Overview in World of Warcraft: Forever

The recommended level for Dire Maul Warpwood Quarter is between 52 and 60. In-game players usually refer to the Warpwood Quarter as "Warpwood," however, some also call it "Dire Maul East." 

## Location of Dire Maul Warpwood Quarter in World of Warcraft: Forever

Dire Maul is north of The High Wilderness in Feralas [zone=357]. It is easily accessible to both Alliance and Horde players.

Dire Maul entrance

Alliance players can fly into Thalanaar, at the eastern border of Feralas where it meets Thousand Needles [zone=400], and travel West along the main road, being sure to skirt around Camp Mojache. Alternatively, Alliance can reach Dire Maul by flying in to Feathermoon Stronghold, taking the boat or swimming directly east, and following the road East to Dire Maul.

Horde players can fly into Camp Mojache and travel West along the main road. 

When you're partway across the The High Wilderness, you'll notice a junction to the North. This will lead you into Dire Maul.

To reach the Warpwood Quarter, make your way through the complex to the Maul arena. The portal to the Warpwood Quarter is in a recess on the East wall. There is also a secret entrance behind a door at Lariss Pavillion.

As with many World of Warcraft: Forever Dungeons, wiping (the entire group dying) in Dire Maul Warpwood Quarter can be a frustrating time. 

Ideally, if the entire group is not dead, you should be able to get a rez (resurrection). This is a spell someone casts on you to bring your character back to life with a small amount of health and mana. Priests and Shamans learn this early; Paladins need to complete a quest to learn resurrection and may not have it. Druids only have an in-combat resurrection spell and are unlikely to train it before Dire Maul Warpwood Quarter.

If you can't get a rez, click to release your spirit, then run back to the dungeon from the Spirit Healer as a ghost. You collect your body when you enter the instance.

## Noteworthy Dire Maul Warpwood Quarter Trash Mobs in World of Warcraft: Forever

### The Crescent Key
Once inside, just to your left you'll see Pusillin. Speak with him to begin a mini-event to follow him halfway through the dungeon, fighting off packs of mobs, where you'll eventually fight him and loot Crescent Key [item=18249]. This key will grant you access to the other Dire Maul instances.

On your way to catch Pusillin, you'll encounter mobs such as Warpwood Tangler [npc=11464] and Warpwood Treant [npc=11462], who are linked together and cannot be pulled separately. Freezing Trap [spell=1499] comes in handy here to help CC. 

Warpwood Crusher [npc=13021] and Phase Lasher [npc=13196] are not linked together and can be pulled separately if you're careful.

Along the chase, you'll encounter many different Wildspawn Satyr [npc=11451], some of which are stealthed, so be alert for those! A Warlock {/forever/class=9/warlock} can easily Banish [spell=8994] if they get out of hand.

### Warpwood Pods
As you travel throughout Dire Maul [zone=2557], you may encounter Warpwood Pod [object=179526]. If you step close to one, you may get rooted or poisoned. However, some of them have a chance to contain Thornling Seed [item=18297] and Runn Tum Tuber [item=18255], used to cook Recipe: Runn Tum Tuber Surprise [item=18267], dropped from Pusillin.

## Dire Maul Warpwood Quarter Bosses in World of Warcraft: Forever

Pusillin [npc=14354] is a level 56-57 elite imp mini-boss. He drops Crescent Key [item=18249].

Abilities

- Blast Wave [spell=17145]
- Fire Blast [spell=14145]
- Fireball [spell=15242]
- Spirit of Runn Tum [spell=22735]

Strategy
Once you engage Pusillin [npc=14354], he will summon four (4) Wildspawn Imp [npc=13276] to his side. Once your tank grabs aggro on Pusillin [npc=14354], your group can AoE down the imps. Pusillin [npc=14354] casts a variety of Fire spells - Kick [spell=1769], Counterspell [spell=2139] and other interrupts can prevent his casts from going off. Casters: watch out for his Spirit of Runn Tum [spell=22735], which is a self-buff increasing his size, melee haste by 20% and chance to reflect spells by 50%.

Quests
Pusillin drops Book of Incantations [item=18261] for Pusillin and the Elder Azj'Tordin [quest=7441]. If you are turning in the quest, some groups will clear the rest of the room and zone out in the tunnel to your east. Once you exit the instance, you'll find yourself in the Lariss Pavilion. Feel free to turn in the quest and zone back in.

Once you zone back in, you'll want to clear your way through the southern passage, along the eastern wall. In the southeast corner, you'll find a ramp going up, as well as a path going down. Take the ramp up to find Lethtendris [npc=14327].

Lethtendris [npc=14327] is a blood elf Warlock {/forever/class=9/warlock}, accompanied by her imp Pimgib [npc=14349].

Abilities

- Void Bolt [spell=22709]
- Shadow Bolt Volley [spell=14887]
- Immolate [spell=20787]
- Curse of Tongues [spell=13338]
- Curse of Thorns [spell=16247]
- Enlarge [spell=22710]

Strategy
Lethtendris [npc=14327] has a plethora of Warlock {/forever/class=9/warlock}-related spells. Her Void Bolt [spell=22709] is a 4-second cast and should be interrupted, as should most of the spells you see casting. She afflicts a couple of curses, so if you have a Mage {/forever/class=8/mage} or Druid {/forever/class=11/druid}, be sure to have them decurse the group. Her imp, Pimgib [npc=14349] also casts a variety of fires pells. He has about 1/3 of her HP, so focus down the imp first then move on to Lethtendris [npc=14327].

Since you went up the ramp to encounter Pusillin [npc=14354], trace your steps back and now choose the tunnel leading downward. In the center of the room, you'll find a pool where Hydrospawn [npc=13280] resides. Clear the mobs in the room carefully, and get ready to fight Hydrospawn [npc=13280].

Hydrospawn [npc=13280] is a water elemental boss.

Abilities

- Riptide [spell=22419]
- Submersion [spell=22420]
- Massive Geyser [spell=22421]
- Summon Hydroling [spell=22714]
- Hydrojet [spell=22715]

Strategy
Hydrospawn [npc=13280] could be approached in 2 ways. Burn him down & ignore the Hydroling [npc=14350] that he spawns, or AoE down the Hydroling [npc=14350] then switch over to Hydrospawn [npc=13280]. The Hydroling [npc=14350] do tend to cause more damage than Hydrospawn [npc=13280] himself, so if you choose to just burn him down quickly, keep an eye on your healers and interrupt spells when you can.

Now that Hydrospawn [npc=13280] is dead, it's time to fight Zevrim Thornhoof [npc=11490]. Move to the tunnel in the northeast corner of Hydrospawn [npc=13280]'s room.

Zevrim Thornhoof [npc=11490] is a satyr boss. He is required to kill in order to access The Lower Conservatory portion of the instance.

Abilities

- Intense Pain [spell=22478]
- Sacrifice [spell=22651]

Strategy
Zevrim Thornhoof [npc=11490] will periodically teleport a party member to his ritual alter and cast Sacrifice [spell=22651]. Healers: whoever is targeted by this will need BIG heals! His other spell, Intense Pain [spell=22478] is like an AoE Shadow Word: Pain [spell=10894] that can be dispelled. Ranged, stand max distance to avoid this. If you have a Warlock {/forever/class=9/warlock} in your group, one tactic is to enslave a Wildspawn Hellcaller [npc=11457] or Wildspawn Shadowstalker [npc=11456] and use them to attack Zevrim Thornhoof [npc=11490].

Once Zevrim Thornhoof [npc=11490] is dead, you can now speak to Old Ironbark [npc=11491] to access The Lower Conservatory.

Old IronbarkNow that Zevrim Thornhoof [npc=11490] is dead, it's time to advance to the final portion of the instance: The Lower Conservatory. Head on back to the courtyard and you'll find Old Ironbark [npc=11491]. Clear your way through the path and watch out for any patrols in the area. Speak to him and he will assist you in opening the next passage for you. Take that passage to the north.

You'll find yourself in a large circular room. Clear the Warpwood Crusher [npc=13021] along the path leading down, and the rest of the trash on the way. Make sure all trash is cleared before you engage the final boss, Alzzin the Wildshaper [npc=11492], or else they will aggro during the fight.

Alzzin the Wildshaper [npc=11492] is a shape-shifting demon boss.

Satyr Form Abilities

- Enervate [spell=22661]
- Wither [spell=22662]
- Thorns [spell=22128]

Bear Form Abilities

Strategy
Alzzin the Wildshaper [npc=11492] will shape-shift into different forms. When he shape-shifts, make sure your tank reestablishes aggro before DPSing.

In his initial, Satyr Form, be sure to cleanse the poison Enervate [spell=22661] right away as it acts as a periodic mana drain. Be sure to cleanse the disease spell, Wither [spell=22662], as well. Both can be interrupted, so watch out for his spell casting.

When he shifts into treant form, his armor increases by a lot, so using like  [spell=11599] and Expose Armor [spell=11198] will help you get through this phase. 

He can also shape-shift into a Dire Wolf, casting abilities similar to a feral Druid {/forever/class=11/druid} in Cat Form [spell=768]. At 35% health, the wall will crumble behind him and a group of non-elite adds will come running out. They're pretty easy to AoE down and finish burning down Alzzin the Wildshaper [npc=11492].

Now that he is defeated, if you have the quest Shards of the Felvine [quest=5526], loot your Felvine Shard [item=18501] from behind his hut. As you exit in the tunnel ahead, there are typically 2-3 Rich Thorium Vein [object=175404] inside. Hopefully you have a Miner in your group to take advantage of those.

### Isalien

Isalien [npc=16097] can be summoned at Alzzin the Wildshaper [npc=11492]'s room in Dire Maul East with the Brazier of Beckoning [item=22050] provided by one of the quests to kill her.

If you don't get any of the quests that require you to kill her (as you receive two out of four random quests), you will be able to summon her in the future after completing the questline with the Brazier of Invocation [item=22057] gifted to you at the end of the questline.
