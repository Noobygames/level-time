 The Alcaz Operative Insignia is a secret toy in World of Warcraft: Forever that gives you a Defias disguise. It starts in the new Alcaz Prison dungeon and takes you to Westfall, then to either Booty Bay or Stormwind depending on your faction. All the information for this secret has come from The WoW Secret Finding Discord . WoW Secret Finding Discord Alcaz Operative Disguise Toy in WoW: Forever The Alcaz Operative Disguise is a toy that transforms you into a member of the Defias. To build it, you need six Fractured Insignia . You earn one by completing this quest chain, and the other five can be bought or traded from other players. The Alcaz Operative Disguise toy Step 1: Free Mason Connery in Alcaz Prison When you run the Alcaz Prison dungeon, do not kill the first boss, The Questioner . Skip past him and clear the rest of the dungeon. At the end of the dungeon, a prisoner named Mason Connery offers a quest to go back, kill The Questioner , and bring him the key to his freedom, promising riches in return. Kill The Questioner, loot the key, and bring it back to Mason Connery to complete the quest. Mason Connery at the end of Alcaz Prison Step 2: More Valuable than Gold in Westfall Mason Connery then gives you the quest More Valuable than Gold , along with a note that reads "WF, 43.4, 67.8". These coordinates lead to the inn at Moonbrook in Westfall. Go to the second floor of the inn and find the box with interactable floorboards. Turn in the quest there to receive a Document Box . Warning: the Document Box vendors for 25 gold. Do not sell it! You need it for the next step. /way Westfall 43.4, 67.8 Moonbrook Inn (second floor) Floorboards (Moonbrook Inn, second floor) Step 3: State Secrets in WoW: Forever This step differs by faction. Both NPCs offer the same quest, State Secrets , which rewards a Fractured Insignia , some gold and experience. Renzik "The Shiv" Bring the Document Box to Renzik "The Shiv" in the SI:7 building in Old Town, Stormwind City, and accept State Secrets . Stormwind City, Old Town Renzik "The Shiv" Rix Xizzix Bring the Document Box to Rix Xizzix in Booty Bay, who is near the Auction House, and accept State Secrets . Booty Bay, near the Auction House Rix Xizzix Step 4: Combine the Fractured Insignia The Fractured Insignia is tradeable, so you do not need to run the chain six times. Buy the other five from other players, or trade them with friends. Once you have six, combine them to create the Alcaz Operative Disguise toy.

How to Find the Hidden Ledge in Blackrock Depths in WoW: Forever Step 1: The Bridge East of the Ring Just east of the Ring in Blackrock Depths is a bridge that leads north over the lava. As you get onto the bridge, look down and to the left. You will see a friendly Dark Iron dwarf below you on the base of a pillar. Clear the rest of the mobs ahead of you on the bridge (Blazing Fireguards and a group of Shadowforge dwarves), then get ready for some parkour. It's a long way down... Step 2: Jump onto the Platform Move to the left side of the bridge, near a small brazier. Get onto the railing, hug the rock wall, and jump ahead onto the platform near the wall. Step 3: Into the Rock Corner Move to the end of the platform, still hugging the wall, and move hard into the rock corner. Step 4: Drop onto the Rock Outcrop Turn with your left shoulder to the wall and run carefully against it, until you fall into the exposed corner. Drop down onto the rock outcrop below. Thankfully, the hard part is over! Video walkthrough from SeventeenUncles Bonus: The Shortcut to Lord Incendius in WoW: Forever There is also a door. Follow the ledge to the end, and carefully jump onto the bridge below.

It comes from an instant turn-in quest offered by a slain Night Watch Guard hidden in Duskwood, and it is also needed for the Ancient Heirloom secret quest chain on Zephras Isle. This guide covers where to find the guard, what the torch does, and where it is used. How to Get the Night Watchman's Torch in WoW: Forever Find the slain Night Watch Guard in Duskwood. Although the guard is dead, it still offers a quest, An Unfortunate End . The quest is an instant turn-in. You just need to accept it and hand it straight back to the guard to receive the Night Watchman's Torch . WoW Secret Finding Discord The Ancient Heirloom is the starting point of one of the newest secret quest chains in World of Warcraft: Forever. It begins with a simple ring on Zephras Isle and leads across Kalimdor, through Darkshore, Ashenvale, Feralas, Azshara, Winterspring and Hyjal, in search of a way to cleanse a befouled water globe. This guide walks through every known step in order, with a map for each location. While many steps can be completed at a lower level, you will likely need to be level 40 or higher to complete the chain in full. This secret is still being solved by the community. Every step in this guide has been completed by players, but the chain does not have a confirmed ending yet. This guide will be reviewed and updated regularly.  Welcome to Wowhead's WoW: Forever bags and storage guide! In this guide, we will talk about everything you need to know about bags and storage in World of Warcraft: Forever. What are Bags in WoW Forever? A bag is defined as a container item that a character can use to hold other items. All characters start with a 16-slot Backpack bag that cannot be moved or replaced, and they have four other slots that can be occupied by bags they find throughout the world. Bags can be obtained from many sources, including quest rewards, crafting professions such as Tailoring , and world drops from mobs and chests. Many bags do not bind to the character and can be exchanged between players or purchased/sold on the Auction House. Bags that are not currently equipped can be stored in your inventory, but bags inside of other bags must be empty - they do not nest more than two deep. Inventory space is an important resource, as more space will allow players to carry more items and make it easier to manage their inventory. This is especially critical for classes such as Warlock s and Hunter s that utilize Soul Shard s and ammunition respectively, and special bag types exist for these classes (such as Felcloth Bag and Quickdraw Quiver ). It is also worth mentioning that many items in Forever stack differently compared to the current iteration of the game (where many items can stack up to 200). For example, herbs such as Dreamfoil stack up to 20, and ore such as Mithril Ore only stack up to 10. In addition, many crafted consumables such as Elixir of the Mongoose , Major Healing Potion , and Flask of the Titans only stack up to 5. Openable items such as Big-mouth Clam do not stack at all. It can be jarring to adjust to these different stacking values, which further highlights the importance of bag space and inventory management.
