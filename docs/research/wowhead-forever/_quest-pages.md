## Findings from spell/NPC pages
- NPC pages (e.g. npc=3653 Kresh) and spell pages return almost no data via WebFetch (JS rendered; curl gets 403): NPC pages give no abilities or levels.
- Cleave spell 5532 and 15496 are two separate Forever spells (both instant physical weapon-damage "next swing", 10, 6 s cooldown, 5 yd). 15496 is linked from a Deadmines guide, so IDs are per-NPC variants, not ranks.
