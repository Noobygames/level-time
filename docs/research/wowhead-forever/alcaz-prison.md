Alcaz Prison [zone=16620], found in the new zone of the Dustwallow Marsh, is one of the new dungeons players will encounter in World of Warcraft Forever.

If you are new to WoW, please look out for this symbol throughout the guide for additional, beginner-friendly tips.

## Alcaz Prison Overview in WoW Forever

Alcaz Prison is a hidden prison on the dangerous Alcaz Island in the zone of Dustwallow Marsh. After learning of the traitorous members of Theramore who aligned themselves with the Defias Brotherhood, it is believed that the missing King of Stormwind, King Varian Wrynn, is being held captive here. While members of the Alliance look to find their lost king and members of the Horde look to save their captive Kor'Kron Guardsmen, the local Naga of the area are also not too happy about the Defias's intrusion here. 

The recommended level for Alcaz Prison is between 49 and 54, and it is a dungeon similar to that of the Stormwind Stockades. Although not too long of a dungeon, there are some challenging encounters here. 

## Location of Alcaz Prison in WoW Forever

The Alcaz Prison dungeon entrance is located on Alcaz Island in Dustwallow Marsh, which is the northeastern island on the map. Players will find the keep on the north side of Alcaz Island after entering from the western side of the island. Once in the keep, the dungeon portal is located in the basement. 

Horde  players should take the flight path to Brackenwall Village and proceed to head to the dungeon. If players do not have the flight path yet, their best option would be to enter from the Ratchet. Alternatively, players can also head south from Ratchet to Alcaz Island.

Alliance players should take the boat from Menethil Harbor to Theramore and proceed to make their way north to the dungeon.

As with many World of Warcraft Forever Dungeons, wiping (the entire group dying) in Alcaz Prison can be a frustrating time. 

Ideally, if the entire group is not dead, you should be able to get a rez (resurrection). This is a spell someone casts on you to bring your character back to life with a small amount of health and mana. Priests, Druids, and Shamans learn this early; Paladins need to complete a quest to learn resurrection and may not have it. 

If you can't get a rez, click to release your spirit, then run back to the dungeon from the Spirit Healer as a ghost. Alcaz Prison's Spirit Healer is located immediately outside the dungeon, so wiping here will not be as frustrating as other dungeons.

## Noteworthy Trash Mobs

### Strashaz Naga
Two powerful Naga at the beginning of the dungeon will be the Strashaz Enchantress [npc=255373] and Strashaz Soldier [npc=255995]. The Enchantresses will Polymorph [spell=13323] random players and cast Frostbolt Volley [spell=12737], and the Soldiers will Berserker Charge [spell=1270802] anyone who's 8 or more yards away from them.

Strategy: Stack up on the rears of the mobs to prevent Berserker Charge [spell=1270802] from going out, and use any interrupts on Polymorph [spell=13323]/Frostbolt Volley [spell=12737]. Be ready to dispel the Polymorph if not interrupted! 

### Alcaz Chaplain
Alcaz Chaplain [npc=255262]s are the healers of this dungeon, and will attempt to Heal [spell=15586] themselves or nearby allies. Their physical counterparts, Alcaz Jailer [npc=271784], Cleave [spell=15496], so tanks should face them away from the rest of the party.

 | 

Strategy: Use crowd-controls such as Polymorph [spell=12825] if more than one Chaplain present. Always focus Chaplains first in trash packs when they're present.

### Defias Truthfinder and Renegade Conjurer
Defias Truthfinder [npc=255271]s and Renegade Conjurer [npc=255364]s are the caster-type mobs located in this dungeon. Truthfinders will cast Shadow Word: Pain [spell=14032] and Mind Flay [spell=17165] on players, while Conjurers have mage spells such as Arcane Missiles [spell=15736], Fireball [spell=14034], and Frostbolt [spell=15497], and will be accompanied by an Arcane Servitor [npc=255369] 

Strategy: First, if presented with 4-5 mobs in a trash pack, we recommend using Crowd Control to keep one off before engaging. Use Line-of-Sight (LoS) areas to bring these mobs to the party. Dispel Shadow Word: Pain [spell=14032] and Mind Flay [spell=17165] from players, and focus interrupts on Fireball [spell=14034] if no Alcaz Chaplains are present.

## Alcaz Prison Bosses in World of Warcraft: Forever

Strategy
Tidemistress Ca'zass [npc=255997] is the first boss of Alcaz Prison and is accompanied by two Shashraz Enchantresses. Players can opt to either take out the adds first or crowd control them and focus the boss. She has a powerful Frostbolt Volley that deals damage to all party members, will magically root players with Frost Nova, and will cast Blizzard on top of herself. After removing the adds out of the equation, move the boss out of Blizzards and take her down. Paladins and Priests should be ready to dispel players from Frost Nova so they are not stuck under a Blizzard.
Abilities | 

 Frost Nova [spell=15532], Frostbolt Volley [spell=8398], Blizzard [spell=1271375]  | 
 

Strategy
Overseer Wrex [npc=255391] patrols all the prison cells in his section of the dungeon. If you aggro Wrex, he'll call for help from all the adds in the 3 prison chambers nearby, so players must sneak past Wrex and clear out all three chambers before starting the encounter. Wrex will patrol and enter the three rooms, so make your way once an opening is available. The boss himself only has one ability: Mortal Strike, a very powerful instant strike to the tank that reduces healing effects by 50% for 5 seconds. He will also Battle Shout himself and any nearby allies once the encounter starts.
Abilities | 

 Mortal Strike [spell=16856], Battle Shout [spell=9128] | 
 

Strategy
WARNING: Do NOT kill this boss before clearing out the rest of the dungeon first! The Questioner [npc=256236] is a very simple boss accompanied by a few adds in his chamber. You can pull some of the adds before pulling the boss to ensure an easy encounter. The boss himself only has two abilities: Rend and Drain Life. Paladin's and Priests can dispel the Drain Life effect, but other than that, a very simple fight.
Abilities | 

 Drain Life [spell=17620], Rend [spell=12054] | 
 

Strategy
Katerina the Cleaner [npc=257016] is accompanied by two Alcaz Chaplains. Use crowd control such as Polymorph [spell=12825], Sap [spell=2070], and Fear [spell=6213] to keep these mobs out of the equation. If you can't, take them down first to prevent any healing from going out. Katerina herself has a couple of very powerful abilities: Grievous Garrote and Deadly Poison. She'll pick a random player and deliver her Garrote to them, dealing powerful instant damage and continuously removing health from the player until they are healed to above 90% HP. Deadly Poison is applied to the primary threat target and needs to be dispelled asap. 

Abilities | 

 Grievous Garrote [spell=1271761], Deadly Poison [spell=3583]  | 
 

Strategy
Zarla Ober [npc=255707] is the final encounter of the dungeon and is a step up in damage output. This encounter has two phases. Phase 1 is the Human phase, and Phase 2 is the Dragon phase, fighting Zarla's true form: Blazeroar [npc=255702]. In Phase 1, Zarla has two abilities: Fireball and Flamestrike. She will continue to cast her Fireballs on top of Flamestrike, so melee players may have to take that damage. When taken to 30% health, she'll cast Reveal Form, turning into Blazeroar and returning to 100% HP. While in this form, Searing Scales will deal consistent damage throughout. She'll cast Flame Breath on the tank, dealing a very high amount of fire damage on the primary threat target. She can also Thrash, dealing multiple auto attacks at once on the tank, and will AoE Fear everyone but the tank. The tank should have a defensive cooldown, Major Healing Potion [spell=1244436], or Major Healthstone [item=9421] from a Warlock while this is happening. Shaman's should place Tremor Totem [spell=8143]. down if present. 
Abilities | 

 Phase 1: Fireball [spell=12466], Flamestrike [spell=18399] , Reveal Form [spell=1275847]. 
Phase 2: Flame Breath [spell=9573], Fear [spell=1271593], Searing Scales [spell=1271604], Thrash [spell=3391]  |
