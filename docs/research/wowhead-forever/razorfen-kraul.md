Razorfen Kraul [zone=491] is located in the Barrens. This guide provides tips and strategies for completing Razorfen Kraul, including everything you need before and during the dungeon, quests to complete inside, boss strategies, loot, and more.

If you are new to WoW: Forever, please look out for this symbol throughout the guide for additional, beginner-friendly tips.

## Razorfen Kraul Overview in World of Warcraft: Forever

Ten thousand years ago - during the War of the Ancients- the mighty demigod, Agamaggan, came forth to battle the Burning Legion. Though the colossal boar fell in combat, his actions helped save Azeroth from ruin. Yet over time, in the areas where his blood fell, massive thorn-ridden vines sprouted from the earth. 

The recommended level for Razorfen Kraul is between 30 and 40. In-game players usually refer to Razorfen Kraul as "RFK,"; however, some also call it "Kraul." 

## Location of Razorfen Kraul in World of Warcraft: Forever

Razorfen Kraul is located in the southern part of The Barrens [zone=17]. The instance portal is just before the Great Lift to Thousand Needles.

Entrance to Razorfen Kraul
Ratchet
Camp Taurajo
Theramore

Alliance players can take the boat from Booty Bay to Ratchet or from Theramore in Dustwallow Marsh. Both will have their challenge. To get to Ratchet, players must run all the way through Stranglethorn Vale from Duskwood. Theramore is easy to get to, as you can take a boat from Menethil Harbor in the Wetlands, but you must cross through the more dangerous Dustwallow Marsh at a low level.

Horde players can fly to Camp Taurajo and then head to the southern part of the Barrens, turning west just before the Great Lift.

As with many World of Warcraft: Forever Dungeons, wiping (the entire group dying) in Razorfen Kraul can be a frustrating time. 

Ideally, if the entire group is not dead, you should be able to get a rez (resurrection). This is a spell someone casts on you to bring your character back to life with a small amount of health and mana. Priests and Shamans learn this early; Paladins need to complete a quest to learn resurrection and may not have it. Druids only have an in-combat resurrection spell and are unlikely to train it before Razorfen Kraul.

If you can't get a rez, click to release your spirit, then run back to the dungeon from the Spirit Healer (marked on the map below) as a ghost. You collect your body when you enter the instance.

Blueleaf Tubers [quest=1221] is a quest chain that begins and ends with Mebok Mizzyrix [npc=3446] in Ratchet. 

The quest requires you to use the Crate With Holes [item=5880], Snufflenose Owner's Manual [item=5897], and Snufflenose Command Stick [item=6684] to loot 6 Blueleaf Tuber [item=5876] from RFK. The 3 items can be found next to Mebok outside the general store in Ratchet.

Once you've acquired the items you need, head into RFK. From there, use the Crate With Holes [item=5880] to summon a Snufflenose Gopher that will find the tubers for you. Use the Snufflenose Command Stick [item=6684] on the gopher to have it search the area for tubers. Rinse and repeat this step until all 6 tubers are collected. 

The quest will be tagged as "Complete" once 6 Blueleaf Tubers have been looted.

Once the journal has been looted, speak to Falfindel Waywarder [npc=4048] to pick up the second and final part of the chain, which requires you to loot Razorflank's Medallion [item=5792] from Charlga Razorflank [npc=4421] in RFK. Charlga is the final boss of the dungeon. 

The quest will be tagged as "Complete" once the hand has been looted.

## Noteworthy Razorfen Kraul Trash Mobs in World of Warcraft: Forever

### Razorfen Totemic
Razorfen Totemic [npc=4440] is one of the most annoying mobs in RFK. Their chief ability, Earthgrab Totem [spell=8376], summons an Earthgrab Totem that lasts 30 sec. and periodically immobilizes nearby enemies. If rooted out of position, your group may be at risk of pulling additional mob packs. Deal with this ability by prioritizing the Earthgrab Totem before killing any other mob in a pack. 

### Razorfen Dustweaver
Razorfen Dustweaver [npc=4522] is similar to the Totemic, only instead of a rooting totem, it can cast Enveloping Winds [spell=6728], which is a cyclone-like ability that stuns a target for 10 seconds. If this is cast on the healer, the tank should be wary of pulling too many mobs. The best way to deal with the dustweavers is to pull only 1 at a time and have the hybrid classes prepared to toss a heal onto the tank if necessary. 

### Death's Head Adept
Death's Head Adept [npc=4516] also casts a CC ability, Chains of Ice [spell=113]. Like the totemic, make sure to only pull one of these at a time and have your group positioned away from any additional mobs or patrols. Because they are casters, the Death''s Head Adepts are easy to kill, but can be dangerous if they catch you off guard.

Roogug is a simple encounter with one notable exception: the surrounding mobs. Before engaging Roogug, make sure to clear the area and CC whatever mobs you can prior to engagement. If you have a Warlock with Banish, make sure to use it on the elemental at his side in order to reduce incoming damage onto the tank.
