 World of Warcraft: Forever is a new evolution of WoW Classic. This guide is a compilation of all the new features, dungeons, zones, and more available at release. World of Warcraft: Forever released on November 4th, 2026. New Features in Forever There are many new features and changes at release of World of Warcraft: Forever, including: New Zones in Forever Four new zones are available in addition to all of the existing Classic zones. Zephras Isle is home to the Zephras. This is a starter leveling zone that Skyborne players of both factions will spawn into. A floating isle that can only be accessed by taking one of the new skyboats from Dalaran or Thunderbluff. Following the zone's starting experience questline will take you to around level 12. Trainers here teach and level all professions to at least Journeyman. Zephras Isle Zone Overview The Riverglades is a rugged frontier of rivers, grasslands, trade routes, struggling communities, and old grudges. A contested zone adjacent to Badlands, Burning Steppes, and Redridge Mountains on the eastern coastline of the Eastern Kingdom. Intended for players of both factions between levels 36 and 44. Accessed by road from the Badlands or Redridge Mountains. New ship route from Gadgetzan to Powderfuse Port in the Riverglades. Contains the new Krol'dok Stronghold dungeon for players from level 40 to 45. Riverglades Zone Overview A zone hidden in plain sight between Mulgore and Desolace. In Shen'Dralas , a long-hidden mystery between Mulgore and Desolace becomes a new adventure tied to the Shen’dralar, Eldre’thalas, Dire Maul, and the centaur tribes of the region. Accessed via the Valley of Bones from Desolace in Kalimdor. Previously an inaccessible, empty shelf of terrain in the Vanilla map. New quest chains involving the Gelkis and Magram centaur clans of Desolace. In the aftermath of Archimonde’s defeat, journey through Darkwhisper Gorge to Mount Hyjal , and help shape the future of a recovering land. An endgame leveling zone. Will contain the Barrows Deep raid in the future. Entered via Darkwhisper Gorge in the South of Winterspring. New Dungeons in Forever An additional nine dungeons, designed for various level brackets, are open from launch: Hall of Thanes (Level 13-18) Ruins of Lordaeron (Level 16-22) Excavation Site (Level 26-32) City of Dalaran (Level 28-34) The Drowned City (Level 35-40) Krol'dok Stronghold (Level 40-45) Alcaz Island Prison (Level 48-53) Blackmaw Hold (Level 55-60) Shaper's Terrace (Level 58-60) New Skyborne Playable Race in Forever Forever brings a new playable race: the Skyborne. There are two variants, one for each faction: High Order Skyborne are members of the Alliance, and Windshaper Skyborne are members of the Horde. These two variants are largely the same, sharing their appearance options, three of their four racials, and four of the six classes available. There are six classes available to Skyborne: Warrior Hunter Rogue Druid Horde only : Shaman Alliance only : Mage The Skyborne are descendants of Highborne rebels who fled to the Zephras Isles, where any new Skyborne character's journey will begin. Skyborne Race Overview Professions in Forever Although professions will still be very recognizable to WoW Classic players, in Forever, there are substantial updates to make crafted items more relevant and to encourage and reward players for interacting with professions throughout their journey. There are over 600 new recipes across all professions and level ranges in Forever. Many existing recipes have also received overhauls to make professions more meaningful as you journey to level 60. There are hundreds of new recipes, including Enchanter-crafted Relics. New Feature: Camping In WoW: Forever, players can place a campfire nearly anywhere in the open world; this will create a campsite. Campsites allow any player to rest and gain benefits such as vendors, repairs, tradeskill workspaces, and buffs. At the campsite, each player can place an object from one of their professions to grant a buff to all players at the campsite. All of these buffs are the same as various class buffs and can not be stacked. For example, Herbalists can place an Incense Candle that increases Intellect; players can not have both the Incense Candle buff and a Mage's Arcane Intellect buff. Camping Overview New Skills and Talents in Forever Significant changes have been made to every specialization's talent tree, changing or augmenting their playstyle and increasing the viability of rarely-seen specializations. New skills are available for some specializations, such as the Frost Mage's Ice Lance or the Restoration Druid's Wild Growth . Try out Wowhead's Forever Talent Calculator . Check out our tools and guides showcasing all of the class changes in Forever, including every change to spells and talents. Changes to Races in Forever There are a number of new class and race combinations in Forever; most notably, Dwarves can be Shamans, and Undead can be Paladins, so there are no longer faction-exclusive classes. Racials have received a range of balancing adjustments, and some races have brand new racial abilities. For example, Tauren characters have Plainsrunning , which increases their movement speed over time as long as they keep moving, and Night Elves have Elune's Light , a new cooldown that increases their critical strike chance temporarily. All Racials and Available Class-Race Combinations Visual Changes in Forever Significant enhancements have been made to the rendering technologies, including improved fog, lighting, and water effects, while maintaining the Classic art direction. New Character Models Players can toggle between the original Standard-Definition models and new High-Definition models at any time. Standard-Definition character model. High-Definition character model. Transmogrification in Forever The Transmog System is available in Forever; this allows players to change the visual appearance of their gear. The system has some key differences from other versions of the game: Toggleable: Players can choose to disable transmog, causing them to see everyone in their gear's base visuals. Group-based unlocks: For Uncommon and Rare Bind-on-Pickup drops, the appearance automatically unlocks for all players in the party, regardless of whether they win the roll. This does not apply to Epics - you must obtain Epic drops to unlock their appearance. Locked by Level and Armor-type: Players can only use the appearances of items they are high enough level to use, and the armor type of the worn gear and transmogged gear must match. For example, Leather gear can only be transmogged to look like other pieces of leather gear. PvP in Forever Although there is no arena or rated battlegrounds in Forever, the Classic ranking system has been revamped to be more accessible and offer more rewards. PvP Overview in Forever Darkspear Island Darkspear Island is a new 15v15 Battleground, with many similarities to Eye of the Storm from The Burning Crusade. Players contest control of four positions around the battleground; the more points under a team's control, the faster they gain points. Additionally, taking the respawning flag from the center of the zone to a controlled point grants a burst of points.
