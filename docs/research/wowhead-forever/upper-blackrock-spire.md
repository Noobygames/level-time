#### Pyroguard Emberseer (NPC ids [9816])
Overview: Click the Blackrock Altar to start the fight. The Incarcerators have low health, so killing them is quite easy; just AoE them down. As for Pyroguard, tanks should keep him away from the group to avoid multiple people getting hit with the fire nova. Outside of that, this fight is quite easy.
- Fire Nova [spell=16079]: Inflicts Fire damage to nearby enemies. | note: Casters and healers should be at least 10 yards away from the boss to avoid being hit by this.
- Flame Buffet [spell=16536]: Inflicts 30 Fire damage to an enemy and increases the Fire damage it takes by 30 for 45 sec.

#### Solakar Flamewreath (NPC ids [10264])
Overview: To start the encounter, reach Father Flame by hugging the northern wall tightly as a raid and walking down to the far end. It is wise to clear out a few of the eggs before starting the encounter, but be aware that they will respawn fairly quickly. As such, some groups avoid focusing on this because it is seen as a futile effort. Clear out the dragonspawn before beginning the event. Then, click Father Flame and loot the item. During the event, multiple waves of mobs will come at your raid. The first is the Rookery Hatcher. They will break the eggs, so it is critical to kill these mobs immediately. Have your mages and warlocks deal with the released whelps. The second mob type is the Rookery Guardian. Have your tanks grab these mobs and tank them back against the Eastern wall. The targets have lower priority than the Hatchers. After a few minutes, Solakar will come, which is a simple encounter. Have your tank grab Solakar and position him along the wall. Finish off the remaining whelps, and then have your group focus on Solakar. Have your raid stay at range. He has a war stomp, which can stun the whole raid.
- War Stomp [spell=16727]: Inflicts normal damage plus 50 to nearby enemies, knocking them back and stunning them for 5 sec. | note: Casters and healers should stay at range to avoid being hit by this ability.

#### Goraluk Anvilcrack (NPC ids [10899])
Overview: This fight is a basic encounter. Tank him against the wall, away from your group.

- Heroic Strike [spell=25710]: A strong attack that increases melee damage by 11 and causes a high amount of threat.

#### Warchief Rend Blackhand & Gyth (NPC ids [10339, 10429])
Overview: Complete the gauntlet to beckon Rend and Gyth. The gauntlet consists of numerous waves of orcs and dragonspawn. After seven to eight waves, Rend will appear riding on Gyth’s back. To combat Gyth, the MT should face Gyth away from the raid to protect them from the breath. At 20%, Rend will be knocked off Gyth’s back. The raid should kill Gyth before switching to Rend. Your OT should immediately grab Rend as soon as he is knocked off Gyth, and position him to the side of the room. Once Gyth is slain, the raid will switch to Rend. Melee should attempt to avoid his whirlwind and cleave mechanics. He hits quite hard, so healing here is the biggest issue with killing Warchief Rend Blackhand, as mana is already low going into this portion of the encounter.

#### The Beast (NPC ids [10430])
Overview: The Beast is a decently difficult fight because of a few mechanics. The first is a charge at a random target. As soon as the charge happens, he will return to the top threat target. The tank should always have the most threat to ensure it returns properly. The second mechanic that will cause issues is the fear; The Beast will occasionally fear nearby players. Ranged and healers should be at max range and spread out to avoid this mechanic. Finally, there is a fire nova ability called Flamebreak that will do fire damage and cause a knockback. Tanks should have their back to a wall to avoid being knocked back. Ranged and healers should be at max range to avoid taking too much AoE damage.
Role notes: tank: Keep your back to a wall to avoid being knocked back.; healer: Stay spread out at max range.; damage: Ranged DPS should stay spread out at max range.

#### General Drakkisath (NPC ids [10363])
Overview: General Drakkisath is the most difficult boss by far. The most common approach is the kite method, which many groups with Hunters will use. It is significantly easier than the other method. In the kiting method, the Hunter will pull the General and kite him back through into the Beast’s room. Once there, the Hunter will feign death, returning Drakkisath to the raid. While this is going on, each tank will grab a Chromatic Elite Guard and burn it down. The MT will then be prepared to grab General Drakkisath. Face him away from the raid to stop Flame Breath from dealing unnecessary damage. General Drakkisath is easy on his own apart from the guards, which is why this method is favored amongst raid groups. The second strategy is similar, but without the kite. Your group can tank both the adds and General Drakkisath at the starting position. The goal is to AoE Cleave the Chromatic Elite Guards down quickly. A possible assist here is to chain fear one of the guards with a Warlock, which lessens the healing burden. Either way, this method is significantly harder at lower gear levels because of the damage output between the guards and General Drakkisath. Once the guards are dead, just as with the kiting method, General Drakkisath is fairly easy on his own.

## Guide text
Source: https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide=35281

Blackrock Spire [zone=1583], located in Blackrock Mountain, is divided into two dungeons, Upper and Lower. This guide provides tips and strategies for completing Upper Blackrock Spire, including everything you need before and during the dungeon, quests to complete inside, boss strategies, loot, and more.

If you are new to WoW: Forever, please look out for this symbol throughout the guide for additional, beginner-friendly tips.

## Upper Blackrock Spire Overview in World of Warcraft: Forever

The recommended level for Upper Blackrock Spire is between 55 and 60. In-game players usually refer to Upper Blackrock Spire as "UBRS,"; however, some also call it "Ubers." 

## Location of Upper Blackrock Spire in World of Warcraft: Forever

Blackrock Spire is located within Blackrock Mountain [zone=25]. Alliance players will fly to Thorium Point in Searing Gorge or Morgan’s Vigil in Burning Steppes. Horde players will fly into Kargath in Badlands, to Thorium Point in Searing Gorge, or to Flame Crest in Burning Steppes.

When entering from Searing Gorge [zone=51], follow the path into the Molten Span. To the East, there is a long chain that runs from a stone dwarf down to a central platform. From Burning Steppes [zone=46], follow the path up to the left into the Molten Span. This path will run to the stone dwarf and his chain. Run up the chain and follow the ledge around the corner. When you reach the edge of the ledge, you can see a balcony just below the edge. Jump onto this balcony. Through the doorway is the instance portal for Blackrock Spire.

Once you zone in, you will be able to access both Upper and Lower Blackrock Spire. The first room has two ramps that lead up to a bridge area. The left side will take you to UBRS, while the right side of the bridge takes you to LBRS.

As with many World of Warcraft: Forever Dungeons, wiping (the entire group dying) in Upper Blackrock Spire can be a frustrating time. 

Ideally, if the entire group is not dead, you should be able to get a rez (resurrection). This is a spell someone casts on you to bring your character back to life with a small amount of health and mana. Priests and Shamans learn this early; Paladins need to complete a quest to learn resurrection and may not have it. Druids only have an in-combat resurrection spell and are unlikely to train it before Upper Blackrock Spire.

If you can't get a rez, click to release your spirit, then run back to the dungeon from the Spirit Healer as a ghost. You collect your body when you enter the instance.

## Upper Blackrock Spire Layout in World of Warcraft: Forever

First Floor: Dragonspire Hall

Second Floor: Hall of Binding and The Rookery

Third Floor: Hall of Blackhand, Blackrock Stadium The Furnace, and Spire Throne

The raid will start within Dragonspire Hall. After extinguishing each of the seven runes located within each alcove, the doors in the northern corner of the hall will open up into a small hallway. 

This hallway will lead to the Hall of Binding. Kill each Blackhand Incarcerator around the room, which unlocks Pyroguard Emberseer [npc=9816]. After killing the Pyroguard, the door to the Rookery will open. 

The Rookery will contain the Solakar Flamewreath Event. Most groups choose to avoid this boss; however, if doing the event, hug the left wall and run all the way to the end to reach the event trigger. Once you reach the end, break a few eggs to release some whelps prior to beginning the encounter to make the fights easier. Then, have your tank start the event at Father Flame. After a few waves, Solakar Flamewreath [npc=10264] will come. This is a simple encounter spanning a few waves of adds. 

Once complete, work your way back to the main door, hugging the wall as you go. To go to the next floor, continue past the doorway, hugging the West wall. Continue around the edge of the room. There is a small gap within the ramp and the broken stone structures that the raid can squeeze through without pulling large numbers of whelps.

Once on the landing above The Rookery, kill all the mobs. Make sure everyone has their back to a wall; otherwise, they will get knocked into the lower level by charging mobs, which will inevitably cause numerous whelps to spawn as their eggs are broken. 

Slowly work through all the packs in the Hall of Blackhand. Once all the mobs are killed, groups can pull Goraluk Anvilcrack [npc=10899]. In this room, Jed Runewatcher [npc=10509] will be present as an optional boss.

After Anvilcrack is slain, players should go up the ramps on the Eastern side of the room to enter Blackrock Stadium. Drop into the Stadium at the Southern Edge to avoid immediately starting the Gauntlet.

Warchief Rend Blackhand's encounter is a lengthy gauntlet, so this is a great time to re-buff and obtain mana before starting. Complete the Gauntlet by having your tanks start the event at the Northern Gate, then killing all the waves, which beckons Gyth [npc=10339] and Warchief Rend Blackhand [npc=10429].

Once the event is completed, the Southern Gate will open. The next room forks in two directions. The right path leads back to the upper level of the Stadium. The left path leads to The Furnace. 

Turn left up the ramp then turn towards the right, which opens into The Furnace. After entering The Furnace, to room to the East contains The Beast [npc=10430] encounter. Heading towards the West leads to the Spire Throne.

Often, groups kill The Beast first and then go back through The Furnace to the Spire Throne to kill General Drakkisath [npc=10363].

## Upper Blackrock Spire Bosses in World of Warcraft: Forever

This room is straightforward. Click the Altar to aggro all of the Blackhand Incarcerator [npc=10316] to release the bind on Pyroguard Emberseer [npc=9816]. Once released, kill Pyroguard to advance into The Rookery.

Pyroguard Emberseer Strategy
Click the Blackrock Altar to start the fight. The Incarcerators have low health, so killing them is quite easy. Just AoE them down with mages and locks.

As for Pyroguard, tanks should keep him away from the group to avoid multiple people getting hit with the fire nova. Outside of that, this fight is quite easy. The boss should die fairly quickly.

This room is a major wipe risk because of the eggs on the ground. Most groups avoid the Father Flame [object=175245] event in this room and continue around the West wall to reach the upper landing overlooking The Rookery. However, some groups opt to do the event, which is quite difficult.

Solakar Flamewreath Strategy
 To start the encounter, reach Father Flame by hugging the Northern wall tightly as a raid and walking down to the far end. It is wise to clear out a few of the eggs before starting the encounter, but be aware that they will respawn fairly quickly. As such, some groups avoid focusing on this because it is seen as a futile effort.

Clear out the dragonspawn before beginning the event.

Then, click Father Flame and loot the item. During the event, multiple waves of mobs will come at your raid. The first is the Rookery Hatcher. They will break the eggs, so it is critical to kill these mobs immediately. Have your mages and warlocks deal with the released whelps. The second mob type of the Rookery Guardian. Have your tanks grab these mobs and tank them back against the Eastern wall. The targets have lower priority than the Hatchers.

After a few minutes, Solakar will come, which is a simple encounter. Have your tank grab Solakar and position him along the wall. Finish off the remaining whelps, and then have your group focus on Solakar. Have your raid stay at range. He has a war stomp, which can stun the whole raid. 

Once killed, your raid should hug the wall again and work your way back around the edge of the room and up the landing. AoE whelps as they spawn, but it is possible to avoid cracking multiple eggs by hugging the corner.

This room is straightforward. The boss in this room is Goraluk Anvilcrack [npc=10899] but the optional boss, Jed Runewatcher [npc=10509] might also present within the Hall. Clear all the mobs then kill Goraluk Anvilcrack and Jed if present. Some groups skip this room as both are optional bosses.

Goraluk Anvilcrack Strategy
This fight is a basic encounter. Tank him against the wall apart from your group.

- Backhand [spell=6253]
- Head Crack [spell=3148]

Warchief Rend Blackhand and Gyth Strategy
After seven to eight waves, Rend will appear riding on Gyth’s back. To combat Gyth, the MT should face Gyth away from the raid to protect them from the breath. 

At 20%, Rend will be knocked off Gyth’s back. The raid should kill Gyth before switching to Rend.

Melee should attempt to avoid his whirlwind and cleave mechanics. He hits quite hard, so healing here is the biggest issue with killing Warchief Rend Blackhand as mana is already low going into this portion of the encounter.

The Beast Strategy

- Berserker Charge [spell=19471]
- Immolate [spell=1094]
- Flamebreak [spell=16785]
- Fear [spell=5782]

The Beast is a decently difficult fight because of a few mechanics. The first is a charge that can’t be avoided. As soon as the charge happens, he will return to the top aggroed target. The MT should always have the most aggro to ensure it returns properly.

The second mechanic that will cause issues is the fear. Raid groups should be at max range and spread out from The Beast to avoid this mechanic.

Finally, there is a fire nova ability called Flamebreak that will do fire damage and cause a knockback. Tanks should have their back to a wall to avoid being knocked back. Everyone else should attempt to back at max range to avoid taking too much AoE damage. 

Lord Valthalak
Lord Valthalak [npc=16042] can be summoned at The Beast [npc=10430]'s room with the Brazier of Beckoning [item=22056] provided for the quest Mea Culpa, Lord Valthalak [quest=8995]. 

You will be able to summon him anytime in the future after completing the questline with the Brazier of Invocation [item=22057] gifted to you at the end of the questline. 

This is the final room within Upper Blackrock Spire. General Drakkisath [npc=10363], the final boss, is located here. There are a few patrols within this room as well as stationed mobs. Kill General Drakkisath to complete the raid.

General Drakkisath Strategy

- Cleave [spell=20569]
- Conflagration [spell=16805]
- Flame Breath [spell=9573]
- Thunderclap [spell=8078]

General Drakkisath is the most difficult by far. The most common approach is the kite method, which many groups with hunters will use. It is significantly easier than the other method.

In the kiting method, the hunter will pull General Drakkisath and kite him back through into the Beast’s room. Once there, the Hunter with feign death returning Drakkisath to the raid. While this is going on, each tank will grab a Chromatic Elite Guard and burn it down. The MT will then be prepared to grab General Drakkisath. Face him away from the raid to account for the flame breath. General Drakkisath is easy on his own apart from the guards, which is why this method is favored amongst raid groups. 

The second strategy is similar, but without the kite. Your group can tank both the adds and General Drakkisath at the starting position. The goal is to AoE Cleave the Chromatic Elite Guards down quickly. A possible assist here is to chain fear one of the guards with a warlock, which lessens the healing burden. Either way, this method is significantly harder at lower gear levels because of the damage output between the guards and General Drakkisath. Once the guards are dead, just as with the kiting method, General Drakkisath is fairly easy on his own.
