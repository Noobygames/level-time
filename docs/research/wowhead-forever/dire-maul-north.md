Dire Maul [zone=2557] is a sprawling dungeon hub located in Feralas. This guide provides tips and strategies for completing the Gordoks Commons of Dire Maul, including everything you need before and during the dungeon, quests to complete inside, boss strategies, loot, and more.

## Dire Maul Gordoks Commons Overview in World of Warcraft: Forever

The recommended level for Dire Maul Gordoks Commons is between 54 and 60. In-game players usually refer to the Gordoks Commons as "Commons," however, some also call it "Dire Maul North." 

## Location of Dire Maul Gordoks Commons in World of Warcraft: Forever

Dire Maul is north of The High Wilderness in Feralas [zone=357]. It is easily accessible to both Alliance and Horde players.

Alliance players can fly into Thalanaar, at the eastern border of Feralas where it meets Thousand Needles [zone=400], and travel West along the main road, being sure to skirt around Camp Mojache. Alternatively, Alliance can reach Dire Maul by flying in to Feathermoon Stronghold, taking the boat or swimming directly east, and following the road East to Dire Maul.

Horde players can fly into Camp Mojache and travel West along the main road. 

When you're partway across the The High Wilderness, you'll notice a junction to the North. This will lead you into Dire Maul.

To reach the Gordoks Commons, make your way through the complex to the Maul arena. The portal to the Gordoks Commons is in a recess on the North wall. 

As with many World of Warcraft: Forever Dungeons, wiping (the entire group dying) in Dire Maul Gordoks Commons can be a frustrating time. 

Ideally, if the entire group is not dead, you should be able to get a rez (resurrection). This is a spell someone casts on you to bring your character back to life with a small amount of health and mana. Priests and Shamans learn this early; Paladins need to complete a quest to learn resurrection and may not have it. Druids only have an in-combat resurrection spell and are unlikely to train it before Dire Maul Gordoks Commons.

If you can't get a rez, click to release your spirit, then run back to the dungeon from the Spirit Healer as a ghost. You collect your body when you enter the instance.

## Dire Maul Gordoks Commons Dire Maul Tribute Run Strategy in World of Warcraft: Forever

TRIBUTE RUN REQUIREMENTS

A Tribute Run is considered a hard mode version of Dire Maul North. In a normal Dire Maul run, you will progress normally through the instance, defeating every single Guard (which counts as a boss). However, on a Tribute run, the group must go through a series of tasks to ensure all Guards are alive and only the final boss of the wing, King Gordok [npc=11501], is killed by the end of the run. 

Be mindful that you will get NO LOOT from the Guards, Stomper Kreeg, Captain Kromcrush or Cho'Rush the Observer on a full Tribute Run.

Here is a quick rundown on what needs to be done to ensure each guard stays alive:

- Guard Mol'dar [npc=14326] - Simply skip him by not going down to his area.
He drops Gordok Inner Door Key [item=18268], required to get to the final part of Dire Maul North. Rogues can Pick Lock [spell=1804] the door (300 Lockpicking required), Engineers can open the door with Large Seaforium Charge [item=4398], and Blacksmiths can open the door with Truesilver Skeleton Key [item=15871].

Stomper Kreeg [npc=14322] - Simply skip him by not getting close to him.

- Easily avoidable as his aggro range is severely reduced.

Guard Fengus [npc=14321] - Skip him, the easiest route is going to the right of his area.

- You must sneak to Fengus's Chest [object=179516] and loot the Gordok Courtyard Key [item=18266] without aggroing him.

Guard Slip'kik [npc=14323] - Freeze him by using the Broken Trap [object=179485] near Knot Thimblejack [npc=14338].

- To fix the trap, you need 1 Frost Oil [item=3829] and 1 Thorium Widget [item=15994] to complete A Broken Trap [quest=1193].

Captain Kromcrush [npc=14325] - Fool him by pretending one person from your group is an Ogre bringing prisoners by using a Gordok Ogre Suit [item=18258].

- You can create one Gordok Ogre Suit [item=18258] per run by bringing Knot Thimblejack [npc=14338] 1 Ogre Tannin [item=18240] (looted from Ogre Tannin Basket [object=179499] in the instance), 4 Bolt of Runecloth [item=14048], 8 Rugged Leather [item=8170] and 2 Rune Thread [item=14341] to complete The Gordok Ogre Suit [quest=5518].
- Tailors and Leatherworkers can learn to craft the Gordok Ogre Suit [item=18258] at a reduced cost by talking to Knot Thimblejack [npc=14338]. Both professions use only 1 Ogre Tannin [item=18240], 2 Bolt of Runecloth [item=14048], 4 Rugged Leather [item=8170] and 2 Rune Thread [item=14341] on their versions of the recipe.

Cho'Rush the Observer [npc=14324] - Cannot be avoided and must be off-tanked while the group ensures King Gordok [npc=11501] is killed first.

- Once King Gordok is dead, Cho'Rush will become friendly and stop attacking.

Once King Gordok is dead, you can talk to Mizzle the Crafty [npc=14353], a friendly Ogre that will spawn at King Gordok's location. Mizzle will coronate your entire group as the new King of the Gordok [spell=22799] (and make all the Ogres in the instance friendly to you), and spawn a Gordok Tribute [object=179564]. This chest contains loot and the more Bosses you leave alive, the better the loot in the chest will be.

In addition to the Tribute chest, you are able to talk to each Guard you kept alive to gain powerful buffs that last 2 hours: 

- Guard Mol'dar [npc=14326]: Mol'dar's Moxie [spell=22818] - Stamina increased by 15%.
- Guard Fengus [npc=14321]: Fengus' Ferocity [spell=22817] - 200 Attack Power.
- Guard Slip'kik [npc=14323]: Slip'kik's Savvy [spell=22820] - 3% Spell Critical Strike chance.

Stomper Kreeg [npc=14322] will also sell a variety of drinks like Gordok Green Grog [item=18269] and Kreeg's Stout Beatdown [item=18284], as well as a limited supply of Volatile Rum [item=9260].
