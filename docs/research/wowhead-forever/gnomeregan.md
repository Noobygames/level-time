## Gnomeregan Overview in World of Warcraft: Forever

Located in Dun Morogh, the technological wonder known as Gnomeregan has been the gnomes' capital city for generations. Recently, a hostile race of mutant troggs infested several regions of Dun Morogh - including the great gnome city. In a desperate attempt to destroy the invading troggs, High Tinker Mekkatorque ordered the emergency venting of the city's radioactive waste tanks. Several gnomes sought shelter from the airborne pollutants as they waited for the troggs to die or flee. Unfortunately, though the troggs became irradiated from the toxic assault, their siege continued unabated. Those gnomes who were not killed by noxious seepage were forced to flee, seeking refuge in the nearby dwarven city of Ironforge. There, High Tinker Mekkatorque set out to enlist brave souls to help his people reclaim their beloved city. It is rumored that Mekkatorque's once-trusted advisor, Mekgineer Thermaplugg, betrayed his people by allowing the invasion to happen. Now, his sanity shattered, Thermaplugg remains in Gnomeregan, furthering his dark schemes and acting as the city's new techno-overlord.

The recommended level for Gnomeregan is 29-38. In-game players usually refer to Gnomeregan as "Gnomer".

## Location of Gnomeregan in World of Warcraft: Forever

Gnomeregan, the former Gnome {/forever/race=7/gnome} capital city, is located in Dun Morogh [zone=1]. Gnomeregan is considered one of the more difficult instances to navigate, alongside Uldaman [zone=1337] and Maraudon [zone=2100]. 

Entrance to Gnomeregan

To get to the instance, Alliance players simply travel west from Ironforge [zone=1537].

Horde players can actually teleport to the instance after completing a short quest chain. Head to the Engineering {/forever/skill=202/engineering} shop in Orgrimmar [zone=1637] and speak to Sovik [npc=3413] to get Chief Engineer Scooty [quest=2842] (available at level 20). Take the boat from Ratchet to Booty Bay, and you'll find Scooty [npc=7853] along the docks, on the lower floor close to the inn.

Speak to Scooty [npc=7853] and complete Gnomer-gooooone! [quest=2843] to get beamed up to Gnomeregan with the help of Goblin Transponder [item=9173]. Keep the transponder for future use!

Unlike most dungeons, Gnomeregan has two dungeon entrances located in the Train Depot. The front entrance leads to The Clockwork Run near Viscous Fallout [npc=7079], and the backdoor entrance leads to the Engineering Labs and Crowd Pummeler 9-60 [npc=6229]. Using the backdoor entrance requires either the Workshop Key [item=6893] or a Rogue {/forever/class=4/rogue} with at least 150 Lockpicking {/forever/skill=633/lockpicking}. Horde players teleport in near the elevator area.

As with many World of Warcraft: Forever Dungeons, wiping (the entire group dying) in Gnomeregan can be a frustrating time. 

Ideally, if the entire group is not dead, you should be able to get a rez (resurrection). This is a spell someone casts on you to bring your character back to life with a small amount of health and mana. Priests and Shamans learn this early; Paladins need to complete a quest to learn resurrection and may not have it. Druids only have an in-combat resurrection spell and are unlikely to train it before Gnomeregan.

If you can't get a rez, click to release your spirit, then run back to the dungeon from the Spirit Healer (marked on the map below) as a ghost. You collect your body when you enter the instance.

## Noteworthy Gnomeregan Trash Mobs in World of Warcraft: Forever

At the start, you'll be greeted by infected troggs. Irradiated Pillager [npc=6329]s will cast Radiation Bolt [spell=9771] that can proc a disease on the target, and Caverndeep Ambusher [npc=6207]s patrol the area, dealing a powerful Backstab [spell=7159] to unsuspecting players.

Horde will have to deal with the Gnome safe zone, and the most troublesome NPC will be the Holdout Medic [npc=6392], as they can heal their allies. ALl players will have to deal with Mobile Alert System [npc=7849] throughout once they get to the infected Leper Gnomes, kill them fast, as they will summon more mobs if they sound their alarm long enough. 

Once players reach the final hallway, be VERY careful and hug the left as you make your way to Mekgineer Thermaplugg, as you can accidentally aggro the entire hallway and guarantee a wipe. Hunters should especially be careful with their pets, as that tends to be the number 1 culprit for this issue. In this final stretch, there are two very powerful mobs: Dark Iron Agent [npc=6212]s and Arcane Nullifier X-21 [npc=6232]s. Dark Iron Agents are in groups of 2-4 and hit hard with their daggers, and will place Dark Iron Land Mine [spell=11802]s on the floor that either need to be killed asap or run from. Arcane Nullifier X-21 [npc=6232] cast Reflection Field [spell=10831], so casters be aware and don't accidentally damage yourself. This also effects all nearby allies, and they will cast this multiple times when alive.
