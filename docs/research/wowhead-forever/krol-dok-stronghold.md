Krol'dok Stronghold [zone=16842], found in the new zone of the Riverglades, is one of the new dungeons players will encounter in World of Warcraft Forever.

If you are new to WoW, please look out for this symbol throughout the guide for additional, beginner-friendly tips.

## Krol'dok Stronghold Overview in WoW Forever

Krol'dok Stronghold is the capital city of the Krol'dok Ogre clan. Overlooking the Riverglades, the ogres are joined by the Twilight's Hammer and Shadowhide Gnolls, who look to take out the Bolderok Ogre Clan and Brotherhood of the Horse and seize full control of the Riverglades of the Eastern Kingdoms.

The recommended level for Krol'dok Stronghold is between 40 and 45, and it is an incredibly large dungeon that players should expect to devote a significant amount of time to. 

## Location of Krol'dok Stronghold in WoW Forever

The Krol'dok Stronghold dungeon entrance is located directly North of Sunnyglade in the Riverglades, just east of the capital's closed-off city entrance; players can find the dungeon portal.

Horde  players should take the flight path to Rogm'ar and proceed to head to the dungeon. If players do not have the flight path yet, their best option would be to enter from the Badlands.

Dungeon Entrance

Alliance players should fly to Farholde Keep and proceed to make their way north to the dungeon. If players do not have a path yet, they can enter through Redridge Mountains. 

Farholde Keep
Dungeon Entrance

As with many World of Warcraft Forever Dungeons, wiping (the entire group dying) in Krol'dok Stronghold can be a frustrating time. 

Ideally, if the entire group is not dead, you should be able to get a rez (resurrection). This is a spell someone casts on you to bring your character back to life with a small amount of health and mana. Priests, Druids, and Shamans learn this early; Paladins need to complete a quest to learn resurrection and may not have it. 

If you can't get a rez, click to release your spirit, then run back to the dungeon from the Spirit Healer as a ghost. Krol'dok Stronghold's Spirit Healer is located immediately outside the dungeon, so wiping here will not be as frustrating as other dungeons.

## Noteworthy Trash Mobs

### Twilight Grunt
Twilight Grunt [npc=258042]s are the most dangerous mobs in the dungeon. Alone, these mobs are not too worrisome, but more than one and your melee party members will start to sweat. They have a very powerful AoE Bladestorm [spell=1307066] ability dealing damage to all players within 8 yards, and will Sunder Armor [spell=15572] the primary threat target 

Strategy: Use stuns to prevent these mobs from using their abilities, and use crowd control such as Polymorph [spell=12825] if there are more than two of these mobs in one trash pack.

### Twilight Outriders
Twilight Outrider [npc=258102]s patrol long paths on their warhounds in groups of three throughout the dungeon. As you're clearing, keep an eye out for long roads as more than likely there are these packs patrolling the area. They are Hunter-like mobs who will Shoot [spell=6660], Multi-Shot [spell=14443], and will immobilize their primary threat target for 10 seconds with Hooked Net [spell=14030].

 | 

Strategy: Use Line-of-Sight when pulling these mobs to pull them away from other trash packs. If you have a Paladin in your party, use Blessing of Freedom [spell=1044] on the tank when they get netted, as the mobs will out-range the tank whenever they net them.

### Twilight Peon
Twilight Peon [npc=277611]s are located around the dungeon and will run at an incredibly fast speed to alert mobs of intruders.

Strategy: Use stuns and slows such as Hammer of Justice [spell=5589] and Hamstring [spell=7372] to prevent this mob from being able to alert other mobs of your party's presence in the dungeon. 

### Krol'dok Shaman and Centurions
Krol'dok Shaman [npc=257888]s will cast Lightning Bolt [spell=12167]s and then use Bloodlust [spell=6742] on themselves and their allies. Krol'dok Centurion [npc=257885]s are Warrior mobs who will Mortal Strike [spell=16856] the primary threat target, and will cast Thunderclap [spell=15548] and Demoralizing Shout [spell=16244] to all melee range players.

 | 

Strategy: Take out the Shamans first and then the Centurions. using stuns such as Hammer of Justice [spell=5589] and Cheap Shot [spell=14902]/Kidney Shot [spell=408] to quickly take mobs out. Coordinate priority order of targets versus using AoE here.

## Krol'dok Stronghold Bosses in World of Warcraft: Forever

Strategy
Slavecrusher Grox [npc=258966] is the first boss of Krol'dok Stronghold and controls the Redridge Prisoner [npc=257900]s nearby. He'll cast Bloodlust, increasing his attack speed by 30% for 30 seconds, and will periodically Knockdown the primary threat target for 2 seconds. Throughout the fight, he'll call for nearby prisoners to assist him in combat with Force Compliance. Players should quickly take out any adds, as their non-elite with relatively low health, and then focus the boss. Grox patrols throughout the mines, so clear out the gnolls in a selected area before starting the encounter. 
Abilities | 

 Bloodlust [spell=21049], Knockdown [spell=11428], Force Compliance [spell=1280085]  | 
 

Strategy
Twilight Commander Zarmok [npc=258972] patrols his area surrounded by Twilight's Hammer members. Before engaging this boss, take out these trash mobs until you have an opportunity to pull without any adds getting involved, as this fight has intense raid-wide damage. Zarmok has two primary abilities: Strike and Rending Whirl. Strike deals damage to the primary threat target, and Rending Whirl deals massive damage to anyone within 8 yards of the boss.
Abilities | 

 Strike [spell=12057], Rending Whirl [spell=1280011], Enrage [spell=8599]  | 
 

Strategy
War Handler Ashka [npc=259281] is accompanied by her two pets: Snarlfang [npc=259283] and Terrorhowl [npc=259285]. We recommend taking down the adds first before focusing the boss. Use stuns on the adds such as Hammer of Justice [spell=5589] and Cheap Shot [spell=14902]/Kidney Shot [spell=408] to make it easier. She'll Strike the primary threat target and focus her wolves on a random party member with Command: Kill for 10 seconds. Remove the adds from the encounter and this fight will be relatively easy.
Abilities | 

 Strike [spell=12057], Command: Kill! [spell=1282076] | 
 

Strategy
Nar'gurk [npc=258964] is accompanied by a Shadowhide Curseweaver [npc=258040] and two Twilight Warlock [npc=258100]s. Use crowd-control such as Polymorph [spell=12825], Sap [spell=2070], and Fear [spell=6213] to keep these mobs out of the equation. If you can't, take them down first to alleviate damage. Nar'gurk will cast Shadow Bolt Volley on the party and control a player with Convert. When a player gets converted, they'll need to be taken down to 50% health for the spell to break. Once the adds are taken down, quickly break Converts and take down the boss.

Abilities | 

 Shadow Bolt Volley [spell=1280356], Convert [spell=1280259]  | 
 

Strategy
Bossman Toof [npc=264773] is a two part encounter. When players engage the Krol'dok Centurion [npc=257885]s on the start of the bridge, Bossman Toof will call for periodic waves of two Krol'dok Brute [npc=258687]s to come after the party. Before the boss will be 2 more centurions and one Krol'dok Shaman [npc=257888]. As the summoned brutes are coming, having a Rogue use Distract [spell=1725] can buy your party time to take this first 3-pack of trash down. Once you get to the boss, he's also accompanied by two Shamans. You can crowd control these Shaman to make the encounter easier. Before starting the encounter, let one more wave of Brutes come out so you have time between the next wave. Bossman toof is a Warrior and has three abilities: Mortal Strike, Thunderclap, and Demoralizing Shout. Mortal Strike's health reduction can be fatal with too many adds out, but by himself this fight should be relatively simple. 
Abilities | 

 Mortal Strike [spell=16856], Thunderclap [spell=15548], and Demoralizing Shout [spell=16244]  | 
 

Strategy
Overlord Mugg [npc=258968] is the final encounter of the dungeon, and is accompanied by two Krol'dok Centurion [npc=257885]s. Use crowd control to keep these adds out of the fight as the damage here is significant. If you cannot, then take the adds out one by one before taking down the boss. Overlord Mugg with jump to random players with Crushing Slam, dealing damage and knocking them and anyone nearby back. As the fight goes on, he gains stacks of Mugg's Fury, increasing his attack speed until he swaps to different weapons. He'll swap to Set of Maces and will finish the encounter with Set of Mauls, increasing his damage by 50%. This is why we do not want to push the boss's health before taking out the adds first. 
Abilities | 

 Crushing Slam [spell=1282397], Mugg's Fury [spell=1282387], Set of Maces [spell=1282393], Set of Mauls [spell=1282394]  |
