If the Disciple is killed or the group wipes, the entire event will fail. The event cannot be repeated until the instance is reset and all Lords of the Fang are killed again. To escort him will require you to clear out any remaining adds on the way to the rock, most of which should already be cleared by progressing through the dungeon. Additionally, his Thundercrack ability does a massive amount of damage to nearby targets, making this fight a nightmare for your melee DPS. Mutanus is a group composition check - make sure to have a healthy set of ranged DPS and at least one hybrid healing class aside from your main healer to assist with the thunderclap damage. There are no special tricks for this fight - stay at max range while healing up the group and try to stay alive. This fight is worth the trouble, as the loot is quite good.

## Wailing Caverns Overview in World of Warcraft: Forever

Recently, a Night Elf druid named Naralex discovered a network of underground caverns within the heart of the Barrens. Dubbed the 'Wailing Caverns', these natural caves were filled with steam fissures which produced long, mournful wails as they vented. Naralex believed he could use the caverns' underground springs to restore lushness and fertility to the Barrens - but to do so would require siphoning the energies of the fabled Emerald Dream. Once connected to the Dream, however, the druid's vision somehow became a nightmare. Soon the Wailing Caverns began to change - the waters turned foul and the once-docile creatures inside metamorphosed into vicious, deadly predators. It is said that Naralex himself still resides somewhere inside the heart of the labyrinth, trapped beyond the edges of the Emerald Dream. Even his former acolytes have been corrupted by their master's waking nightmare - transformed into the wicked Druids of the Fang.

The recommended level for Wailing Caverns is between 15 and 25. In-game players usually refer to Wailing Caverns as WC.

## Location of Wailing Caverns in World of Warcraft: Forever

Wailing Caverns is located in the Horde leveling zone of The Barrens [zone=17]. The instance portal is in a cave system in the Lushwater Oasis just southwest of the Crossroads. Wailing Caverns is the first non-linear dungeon of the game, making it a challenge to navigate for new players. However, due to its high-quality agility gear and quest rewards, many players will want to run WC at least once.

Horde players can travel southwest from the Crossroads to find the cavern entrance.

For Alliance players, reaching Wailing Caverns [zone=718] is a bit more of a journey. Travel east from Astranaar in Ashenvale [zone=331]. After you cross the river, take the next road south to the Barrens. Then travel south and west to get to Wailing Caverns. Alternatively, you can take the boat from Booty Bay to Ratchet and head west to the dungeon.

As with many World of Warcraft: Forever Dungeons, wiping (the entire group dying) in Wailing Caverns can be a frustrating time. 

Ideally, if the entire group is not dead, you should be able to get a rez (resurrection). This is a spell someone casts on you to bring your character back to life with a small amount of health and mana. Priests and Shamans learn this early; Paladins need to complete a quest to learn resurrection and may not have it. Druids only have an in-combat resurrection spell and are unlikely to train it before Wailing Caverns.

If you can't get a rez, click to release your spirit, then run back to the dungeon from the Spirit Healer (marked on the map below) as a ghost. You collect your body when you enter the instance.

## Noteworthy Wailing Caverns Trash Mobs in World of Warcraft: Forever

### Druid of the Fang
Druid of the Fang [npc=3840] is the first and only notable trash mob you will come across in the Wailing Caverns. They are hard-hitting casters with some abilities to be wary of. The first ability, Druid's Slumber [spell=8040], will put an enemy target to sleep for up to 15 seconds. The problem with this ability is that it can also target healers, effectively neutralizing them for 15 seconds. They can also heal with Healing Touch [spell=5187], we recommend prioritizing interrupts for the sleep, however, as that is more deadly. Along with these mobs, we recommend having a class that can remove Poison and Disease effects, as they are rampant throughout this dungeon.

Strategy: The best way to deal with the slumber is to ensure you're only fighting one druid at a time (CC all others if nearby). Prioritize and burst down each druid as quickly as possible, and interrupt them when they begin casting their slumber ability. Eventually, the druids will cast Serpent Form [spell=8041], which silences them and negates the possibility of a slumber cast.

The Naralex Event is an optional dungeon event that unlocks when all 4 Lords of the Fang have been slain. During the event, players will be required to escort the Disciple of Naralex [npc=3678] to the ritual stone where Naralex sleeps. From there, the Disciple will summon Mutanus the Devourer [npc=3654], a level 22 Elite Murloc Boss, whose aim is to kill the Disciple. Players must protect the Disciple at all costs while remaining alive. If the Disciple is killed or the group wipes, the entire event will fail. The event cannot be repeated until the instance is reset and all Lords of the Fang are killed again.

Strategy
The first part of the event is simple; escort the Disciple through the Wailing Caverns towards the ritual stone. To escort him will require you to clear out any remaining adds on the way to the rock, most of which should already be cleared by progressing through the dungeon. Once the Disciple reaches Naralex and begins his final ritual, waves of adds will approach the Disciple to kill him. Burn down the adds while keeping your AOE DPS alive. After the 2nd wave of adds, Mutanus will appear to kill your group and the Disciple. He is very challenging, as he possesses two forms of crowd control that can be used against your group.

Additionally, his Thundercrack [spell=8150] ability does a massive amount of damage to nearby targets, making this fight a nightmare for your melee DPS. Mutanus is a group composition check - make sure to have a healthy set of ranged DPS and at least one hybrid healing class aside from your main healer to assist with the thunderclap damage. There are no special tricks for this fight - stay at max range while healing up the group and try to stay alive. This fight is worth the trouble, as the loot is quite good.
