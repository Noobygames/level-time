## Stratholme Live Side Overview in World of Warcraft: Forever

Once the jewel of northern Lordaeron, the city of Stratholme is where Prince Arthas turned against his mentor, Uther Lightbringer, and slaughtered hundreds of his own subjects who were believed to have contracted the dreaded plague of undeath. Arthas' downward spiral and ultimate surrender to the Lich King soon followed. The broken city is now inhabited by the undead Scourge -- led by the powerful lich, Kel'Thuzad. A contingent of Scarlet Crusaders, led by Grand Crusader Dathrohan, also holds a portion of the ravaged city. The two sides are locked in constant, violent combat. Those adventurers brave (or foolish) enough to enter Stratholme will be forced to contend with both factions before long. It is said that the city is guarded by three massive watchtowers, as well as powerful necromancers, banshees and abominations. There have also been reports of a malefic Death Knight riding atop an unholy steed, dispensing indiscriminate wrath on all those who venture within the realm of the Scourge.

The recommended level for Stratholme Live Side is between 55 and 60. In-game players usually refer to Stratholme Live Side as "Strat Live." 

## Location of Stratholme Live Side in World of Warcraft: Forever

Stratholme is divided into two sections, commonly referred to as Strat Undead and Strat Live, based on the mobs present in each half. It is located in the northwest of Eastern Plaguelands [zone=139]. To get there as either Horde or Alliance, fly to Lights Hope Chapel and run northwest. The entrance for Strat Undead is east of the entrance to Strat Live.

Stratholme Live Entrance
Stratholme Undead Entrance

As with many World of Warcraft: Forever Dungeons, wiping (the entire group dying) in Stratholme Live Side can be a frustrating time. 

Ideally, if the entire group is not dead, you should be able to get a rez (resurrection). This is a spell someone casts on you to bring your character back to life with a small amount of health and mana. Priests and Shamans learn this early; Paladins need to complete a quest to learn resurrection and may not have it. Druids only have an in-combat resurrection spell and are unlikely to train it before Stratholme Live Side.

If you can't get a rez, click to release your spirit, then run back to the dungeon from the Spirit Healer as a ghost. You collect your body when you enter the instance.

## Stratholme Live Side Bosses in World of Warcraft: Forever

The first boss you may encounter is Ezra Grimm [npc=11058]. He is located at the green number 6 on the layout map. You must click on the wooden tobacco box in the area to summon him. He has an AoE fire attack and an AoE stun, so spreading out a little can help. He also spawns a bunch of adds with him and will also pull any extra mobs in the area, so make sure they're all dead, or the fight may be a little too hard. If you do happen to wipe, the mailbox will despawn, and you will not be able to summon him again until you reset the instance.
His abilities:

- Flame Breath [spell=17294]
- Smoke Bomb [spell=7964]

Skul [npc=10393] is a rare spawn that you may encounter just before Ezra Grimm [npc=11058] at number 1 on the map. He also has another spawn point over to the east side of the dungeon. His mechanics are very simple, he just casts spells which you should interrupt to reduce damage if you can.
His abilities are:

- Arcane Bolt [spell=15230]
- Frost Shock [spell=15499]
- Frostbolt [spell=16799]

He also has a chance to spawn east past The Unforgiven [npc=10516] along Market Row. He has a sleep which lasts for 8 seconds that he will cast on a random player. He also has a multi-shot ability which can be countered by spreading out a little.
These abilities are:

- Enchanting Lullaby [spell=16798]
