#### Edwin VanCleef (NPC ids [639])
Overview: When you engage Edwin VanCleef, two Defias Blackguards will break stealth and attack you as well. Like Mr. Smite, make sure to deal with these mobs first. CC one and 'skull' mark the other, and burn it down quickly, as VanCleef does a tremendous amount of single-target damage and can shred your tank. Once both of the adds are dealt with, focus back on VC. Once he hits 50% health, he will summon 2 more adds for you to deal with. It remains quite heal-intensive, so the healers and hybrids in your group should be on their toes and provide spot-healing as it is needed. If VanCleef is stunned the moment he reaches 50%, he won't spawn the second wave of adds. Using an ability like Hammer of Justice when the boss reaches 51-55% health will make the fight a lot easier.
Role notes: tank: Kill the Blackguards first. Try to stun VanCleef just before he reaches 50% health.; healer: The tank will take heavy damage, particularly when the Blackguards are still alive at the start of the fight.; damage: Kill the Blackguards first. The Deadmines, situated in Westfall, deep within Alliance territory, is one of the first dungeons many players will encounter in World of Warcraft: Forever.

If you are new to World of Warcraft: Forever, please look out for this symbol throughout the guide for additional, beginner-friendly tips.

## The Deadmines Overview in World of Warcraft: Forever

Originally used to produce gold, during the First War, the Stonemasons left the mines and went to assist Stormwind City, eventually helping to build it. However, when it was time to be paid for the rebuilding work, the King refused any settlement. Riots followed, during which the Queen was killed, and the Stonemasons retreated to The Deadmines, led by Edwin VanCleef and becoming the Defias Brotherhood.

The recommended level for The Deadmines is between 18 and 23. In-game, players usually refer to The Deadmines as "DM", however, some also call it "VC" (as in Edwin VanCleef).

## Location of The Deadmines in World of Warcraft: Forever

The Deadmines can be tricky to find, with the entrance inside a building in Moonbrook, Westfall.

Getting to The Deadmines [zone=1581] is pretty easy for Alliance. Simply fly to Sentinel Hill in Westfall [zone=40] and travel south into Moonbrook. You'll see a mine entrance. Groups usually wait here and gather up before entering the mines. You'll find mobs along the way to the instance portal, so it's always better to go with a group!

For Horde, traveling to The Deadmines [zone=1581] is a lot more time-consuming, but if you are up for the challenge, take the zeppelin to Grom'gol Base Camp in Stranglethorn Vale [zone=33]. Then, most people play it safe by swimming up the coast, north along the shoreline until they hit the southern tip of Westfall [zone=40], surfacing onto the shore and heading to The Deadmines [zone=1581] from there.

As with many World of Warcraft: Forever Dungeons, wiping (the entire group dying) in The Deadmines can be a frustrating time.

Ideally, if the entire group is not dead, you should be able to get a rez (resurrection). This is a spell someone casts on you to bring your character back to life with a small amount of health and mana. Priests and Shamans learn this early; Paladins need to complete a quest to learn resurrection and may not have it. Druids only have an in-combat resurrection spell and are unlikely to train it before Deadmines.

If you can't get a rez, click to release your spirit, then run back to the dungeon from the Spirit Healer (marked on the map below) as a ghost. You collect your body when you enter the instance.

Starting Location
Stormwind City, Dwarven District - Wilder Thistlenettle [npc=656] /way 65 21

What To Do
Collect 4 Miners' Union Card [item=1894] from the elite undead in the mine tunnels outside The Deadmines [zone=1581]. You do not need to enter the dungeon itself for this quest, but the enemies are still elite.

## Noteworthy Deadmines Trash Mobs in World of Warcraft: Forever

### Goblin Engineer and Craftsman
Goblin Craftsman [npc=1731] and Goblin Engineer [npc=622] are two of the most vicious mobs in all of The Deadmines. They can be found in the Goblin Foundry before engaging with Gilnid. They are most notable for their Summon Remote-Controlled Golem [spell=3605] ability, which summons a deadly miniature robot to attack players once cast.

Strategy: You have several ways to deal with Summon Remote-Controlled Golem [spell=3605]. The first option is to stun the goblins while they are casting the ability. Paladins come in handy here as they can simply use Hammer of Justice [spell=853] during the cast. Your second option is to CC all goblins aside from the one you want to prioritize. The issue with this option is that few groups bring more than one mage, so stunning will still be the priority. Finally, if a golem cast does go off, make sure to kill the GOBLIN first, as the golem will despawn once its summoner is dead.

### Defias Squallshaper
Defias Squallshaper [npc=1732] is a caster-type mob that is located in the Dreadnaught room of The Deadmines. They are noteworthy due to their Frost Nova [spell=122] ability, which can be frustrating for tanks and melee.

Strategy: Make sure to have your group stack on the tank while pulling these mobs, so no one gets caught in a deadzone. Also, beware of patrolling Squallshapers as they can catch you out of position and nova you away from your group. Aside from that, these are simple enough.

### Dungeon Patrols
There are many different dungeon patrols in The Deadmines. They often catch groups off guard after boss kills, as most groups will drop their guard while looting, rolling, and eating/drinking.

Strategy: No specific strategy is required. Just make sure to pan your camera around while progressing through The Deadmines, especially after a boss kill, so that you are not caught off guard by a patrol.

### In Case of Emergency
In the two most difficult areas of the dungeon, you have a "get out of jail free card," as there are areas that you can jump to and reset the mobs. The first screenshot is from the top of the Goblin Foundry, and the second one is on the second floor of VanCleef's ship. If the pull is going awry, jump to these locations and wait for the mobs to run back. Even if you're the only person left, you might be able to resurrect the rest of the party if you make it to one of these spots.
