#### Highlord Omokk (NPC ids [9196])
Overview: Highlord Omokk is accompanied by two other Ogres. Most groups tend to CC one of the adds, while the tank picks up Highlord Omokk, keeping him away from the group, with their back against a wall due to Omokk's knockback ability. Decide within your group how to handle the adds. Either CC both if possible and kill Highlord Omokk first, or keep one add CC'd the entire time, kill the other, then Omokk. The choice is yours, but be sure crowd control is involved because it will make this encounter a lot easier. Highlord Omokk himself hits pretty hard, so healers, be sure to keep an eye on the tank's health, especially if it gets low. With his Enrage, he can dish out devastating damage quickly.
Role notes: tank: Keep your back to a wall to avoid being knocked back.

Blackrock Spire [zone=1583], located in Blackrock Mountain, is divided into two dungeons, Upper and Lower. This guide provides tips and strategies for completing Lower Blackrock Spire, including everything you need before and during the dungeon, quests to complete inside, boss strategies, loot, and more.

## Lower Blackrock Spire Overview in World of Warcraft: Forever

The mighty fortress carved within the fiery bowels of Blackrock Mountain was designed by the master dwarf-mason, Franclorn Forgewright. Intended to be the symbol of Dark Iron power, the fortress was held by the sinister dwarves for centuries. However, Nefarian - the cunning son of the dragon, Deathwing - had other plans for the great keep. He and his draconic minions took control of the upper Spire and made war on the dwarves' holdings in the mountain's volcanic depths. Realizing that the dwarves were led by the mighty fire elemental, Ragnaros - Nefarian vowed to crush his enemies and claim the whole of Blackrock mountain for himself.

The recommended level for Lower Blackrock Spire is between 55 and 60. In-game players usually refer to Lower Blackrock Spire as "LBRS," however, some also call it "Elbers ." 

## Location of Lower Blackrock Spire in World of Warcraft: Forever

Blackrock Spire is located within Blackrock Mountain [zone=25]. Alliance players will fly to Thorium Point in Searing Gorge or Morgan’s Vigil in Burning Steppes. Horde players will fly into Kargath in Badlands, to Thorium Point in Searing Gorge, or to Flame Crest in Burning Steppes.

When entering from Searing Gorge [zone=51], follow the path into the Molten Span. To the East, there is a long chain that runs from a stone dwarf down to a central platform. From Burning Steppes [zone=46], follow the path up to the left into the Molten Span. This path will run to the stone dwarf and his chain. Run up the chain and follow the ledge around the corner. When you reach the edge of the ledge, you can see a balcony just below the edge. Jump onto this balcony. Through the doorway is the instance portal for Blackrock Spire.

Once you zone in, you will be able to access both Upper and Lower Blackrock Spire. The first room has two ramps that lead up to a bridge area. The left side will take you to UBRS, while the right side of the bridge takes you to LBRS.

As with many World of Warcraft: Forever Dungeons, wiping (the entire group dying) in Lower Blackrock Spire can be a frustrating time. 

Ideally, if the entire group is not dead, you should be able to get a rez (resurrection). This is a spell someone casts on you to bring your character back to life with a small amount of health and mana. Priests and Shamans learn this early; Paladins need to complete a quest to learn resurrection and may not have it. Druids only have an in-combat resurrection spell and are unlikely to train it before Lower Blackrock Spire.

If you can't get a rez, click to release your spirit, then run back to the dungeon from the Spirit Healer (marked on the map below) as a ghost. You collect your body when you enter the instance.

## Noteworthy Lower Blackrock Spire Trash Mobs in World of Warcraft: Forever
Get ready to jump down onto the ledge to enter LBRS. The first packs of mobs that you'll encounter are packs of orcs, some with Worgs. Watch out for patrols - wait for them to pass before pulling a group. As a general rule of thumb, CC (crowd control) as many adds as possible.

Follow through the hallway, continuing to fight your way through. You'll enter a larger room with a ramp of rubble to your left. Ascend the ramp to the top. If you already have your Unadorned Seal of Ascension [item=12219], you can pick up Seal of Ascension [quest=4742] from Vaelan [npc=10296] now. Continue forward to the bridge. It's a good idea to pull mobs onto the bridge and utilize that area for quite a bit, to avoid runners. 

Take a look around, and you'll find a Roughshod Pike [item=12533]. If you plan to summon and kill Urok Doomhowl [npc=10584], have a member of your group loot one of these pikes.

Move forward, towards the second bridge. Clear the surrounding area as you make your way ahead onto the platform. Ogres and Orcs will be guarding the path to the first boss, Highlord Omokk [npc=9196]. Watch out for their Knockback [spell=26027] abilities - always try to keep your back to a wall or area that won't launch you off the edge!

### Tazz'Alor Trash
The room after Highlord Omokk [npc=9196], Tazz'Alor, is filled with groups of Trolls. The best practice for you is to carefully pull the troll groups into the previous room to avoid runners. There are several patrols walking around, and in that crowded room, it wouldn't be hard to aggro additional mobs. Continue to carefully clear the room and head through the door that leads to a ramp, up and to the right. Pull more mobs down the ramp, slowly and methodically. Remember to keep utilizing LoS (line-of-sight) when casters are involved. You'll make your way to Shadow Hunter Vosh'gajin [npc=9236].

### Orc Trash - Firebrand Invokers
After War Master Voone [npc=9237] is down, head on back into the large room you came from. You'll find a pathway you haven't gone yet - it'll be filled with Orcs. Fight your way down the hallway, watching out for runners. Once you see the lava, turn the corner, and you'll find more mobs, with notably a bunch of casters. Remember to utilize LoS pulling techniques here, interrupting spellcasting when possible. 

Firebrand Invoker [npc=9262] are very dangerous - they're very powerful AoE casters, with spells such as , Fireball [spell=11921], Flamestrike [spell=11829] and Mark of Flames [spell=15128]. Be sure to kill them as soon as possible or keep them CC'd.

Keep left along the path and be extremely careful with your pack pulls. Watch out for the sleeping orcs, too! Things could escalate quickly here if not pulled carefully. This is the area in which rare spawn, Bannok Grimaxe [npc=9596] has a chance to spawn.
