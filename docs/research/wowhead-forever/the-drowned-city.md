Source: https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide=35328

The Drowned City [zone=16550], found on the far western island west of Stranglethorn Vale, is one of the new dungeons players will encounter in World of Warcraft Forever.

If you are new to WoW, please look out for this symbol throughout the guide for additional, beginner-friendly tips.

## The Drowned City Overview in WoW Forever

The Drowned City is the fabled lost Troll city of I'lalai. Inhabited by the remaining trolls and infested with the Saltseer Pirates, and culprit of the Kurzen Compounds magical corruption, adventurers have now found this fabled city and look to stop the dangers that lurk within

The recommended level for The Drowned City is between 35 and 42, and we also recommend players bring a Warlock or Shaman for underwater breathing, as there is a portion of this dungeon that requires extensive time underwater

## Location of The Drowned City in WoW Forever

The Drowned City dungeon entrance is located off the coast of Stranglethorn vale, south of the Haunted Isle, nestled deep in a cave in the ocean.

Horde  players should take the Zeppelin from Durotar to Grom'gol, or take the boat from Ratchet in The Barrens to Booty Bay.

As with many World of Warcraft Forever Dungeons, wiping (the entire group dying) in The Drowned City can be a frustrating time. 

Ideally, if the entire group is not dead, you should be able to get a rez (resurrection). This is a spell someone casts on you to bring your character back to life with a small amount of health and mana. Priests, Druids, and Shamans learn this early; Paladins need to complete a quest to learn resurrection and may not have it. 

If you can't get a rez, click to release your spirit, then run back to the dungeon from the Spirit Healer as a ghost. The Drowned City's Spirit Healer is located immediately outside the dungeon, so wiping here will not be as frustrating as other dungeons.

## Noteworthy Trash Mobs

### Makrura Snapper and Chasm Crawlers
The first mobs players will encounter are Makrura Snappers and Chasm Crawlers. Chasm Crawlers are in packs of 2-3 and hit very hard, and also apply Muscle Tear to the primary threat target, reducing their Strength by 21. Allow your tank to establish threat when pulling these mobs. Makrura Snappers apply stacking Sunder Armor to the primary threat target, reducing their armor

Strategy: Take your time with these trash packs. The way to failure here is simply by pulling too much. The sunder armor and hard hitting Chasm Crawlers can lead to your tank tanking very powerful and intense hits.

### Risen Sentry and Profane Necro
Risen Sentry mobs come in packs of 3-4 and can also have a Profane Necro mob with them. Risen Sentries charge players, knocking them back. Profane Necros conjure a Spirit, summoning a Drowned Spirit into the encounter. 

 | 

Strategy: Stack on top of the mobs to avoid charges, and take down the profane Necros first to prevent them from summoning additional adds. Make sure you are not in a position to have any player knocked into additional mobs

### Brinescale Sorceror
The Brinescale Naga have a few mob types in The Drowned City, but the most powerful are the Brinescale Sorcerers. They will cast hard-hitting Frostbolts and will attempt to Fear players. 

Strategy: Use Line-of-Sight to pull these mobs back, or run back to outrange their Frostbolt range to force them to move towards you and your party. The areas in which these mobs are located are dense with other trash packs, and one wrong Fear can lead to a wipe. If you are limited on interrupts, prioritize interrupting the Fear, but try to get the Frostbolts as well, as they deal a significant amount of damage. If you have a Shaman in your group, make sure a Tremor Totem [spell=8143] is placed during these pulls.

## The Drowned City Bosses in World of Warcraft: Forever

Strategy
Zul'Alai [npc=258905] is the first boss of The Drowned City and deals heavy physical damage to his primary threat target. He'll periodically cast Monstrous Slam, stunning players near him for 3 seconds and Rotted Strike, slowing movement and attack speed to nearby players. Dispell this debuff on your tank first, and then dps players. When he reaches 30% HP, he will gain Undying Oath, increasing attack speed by 30% and regaining 3% health every 5 seconds until defeated. He does not move when casting Monstrous Slam, so melee players can move out to avoid it completely.
Abilities | 

 Monstrous Slam [spell=1282504], Rotted Strike [spell=1282519], Undying Oath [spell=1282511]  | 
 

Strategy
Var'Taka [npc=258924] is accompanied by two Brinescale Priestess [npc=270910]s. The Priestesses will attempt to heal themselves and Var'Taka, and will cast Frostbolt at the primary threat target, so either focus them down first or use crowd control like Polymorph or Sap. Var'Taka will Harpoon random players, spearing them and pulling them to the boss, and has a frontal Cleave, so the tank needs to face him away from the rest of the party. Before you engage this boss, be careful of the trash packs to the left and right. Click on the Glowing Pearl after defeating him to disrupt the barrier
Abilities | 

 Harpoon [spell=1282615], Cleave [spell=15284]  | 
 

Strategy
Primeval Elemental [npc=263886] is a somewhat hidden and optional boss in the Drowned City. When players enter the Deep Archives, they can go right into the water tunnels to find his chamber. When you've spotted the Undying Offering [npc=263899]s, you have found his quarters. Slay all the offerings for the boss to become active. The boss himself only has two abilities: Waterbolt and Freeze Water. The former is a single target ability casted on the primary threat target, and the later is an AoE damage ability. It's a fairly easy encounter, just make sure you don't run out of breath and swim up if you need!
Abilities | 

 Waterbolt [spell=1290978], Freeze Water [spell=1290983]  | 
 

Strategy
Captain Dreadrise [npc=260042] is the leader of the Saltseer Pirates who have infiltrated the troll city. He will Embrine a random player, placing them in an Abyssal Bubble that needs to be killed before it drowns the player. This can be cast on the tank as well. He will also cast Waterbolt Volley, dealing damage to all targets. This can and should be interrupted. Before you pull the boss, make sure you clear out an area completely to avoid pulling any patrolling packs with the boss. Click on the Glowing Pearl after defeating him to disrupt the barrier

Abilities | 

 Embrine [spell=1282842], Waterbolt Volley [spell=1282860]  | 
 

Strategy
Deathless Marrow [npc=269586] is a fight balancing fighting waves of adds and defeating the marrow itself. Deathless Marrow will cast Bloodbolt, dealing damage to all players. Deathless Guardians and Deathless Sorcerors will continue to be summoned throughout the encounter until Deathless Marrow is defeated. Guardians stun players with Shield Slam [spell=1283081], so we recommend taking them out first. Players should cleave off the boss on the adds throughout the fight, as typically the adds will overwhelm the players if they focus on them too much over the boss. Use crowd control's such as Fear [spell=6213], Polymorph [spell=12825], or AoE fears such as Intimidating Shout [spell=5246]/Psychic Scream [spell=8124] when adds get overwhelming.
Abilities | 

 Bloodbolt [spell=1283034], Summon Adds  | 
 

Strategy
Min'loth the Serpent [npc=260243] is a two-phase fight. Phase one: you fight him in his troll form, and when taken to 0% HP, he'll start Phase 2 in his Serpent Form and regain 100% of his health. In Phase 1, Min'loth has two abilities: Drench and Deluge. Drench deals significant damage to his primary threat target, and Deluge is a channeled cast that deals AoE damage to all party members. In Phase 2, he has two new abilities: Fear and Poison Bolt Volley. He'll poison all targets; this needs to be cleansed ASAP, and he will pick a random target to Fear. The fight ends once he reaches 0% in Phase 2.
Abilities | 

 Drench [spell=1283250], Deluge [spell=1283233], Fear [spell=1313471], Poison Bolt Volley [spell=1283313]  | 
 

Strategy
Umbra [npc=264172] is a Rare Spawn Boss that is accompanied by 5 Saltscale Murlocs. These murlocs can apply a Bleed to their targets, and altogether can deal rapid and fatal damage to their targets. Besides that, it appears Umbra themself does not have any mechanics, and is a simple tank and spank. Focus the boss down fast and then take care of the remaining adds

Abilities | 

 Bleed  | 
 

Strategy
Gill [npc=264177] is found along the pathway leading to Primeval Elemental in the Deep Archives. He will Throw Dynamite [spell=1291692] to the primary threat target. It's a fairly simple fight that only has issues should you pull him with the trash nearby,
